Lord Statblocks

Encounter-facing mechanics. Source-locked from Monster Compendium v2.6. Lore and portrayal remain in the Game Master's Guide.

UNIVERSAL LORD RULES

Lord Action Points: Primary 3, Secondary 1, Narrative 0; refresh at start of the Lord's turn. Reactions: normally 2; refresh at start of turn. Lord Resistance: when a Lord fails a Saving Throw, expend one use to succeed instead. Dragon Lords: Change Form is normally a Bonus Action once on their own turn; one continuous HP pool and encounter state; no player Flight Slots.
