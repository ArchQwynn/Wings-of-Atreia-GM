Traits & Signature
Judged — Signature.

Nezekan may have one Judged creature at a time. Once on each of his turns, the first Justice Blade hit against his Judged creature deals +10 Radiant damage. After Justice Unbound triggers, this becomes +15. Judged is a keyword, not a Condition.

Actions
Righteous Sequence. Nezekan makes three Justice Blade attacks.
Justice Blade. +17 to hit, reach 10 ft, one target. Hit: 29 damage (15 Slashing + 14 Radiant).
Sentence. One creature Nezekan can see within 90 ft makes a DC 24 CHA save. Failure: 58 Radiant damage and Speed -20 ft until the end of its next turn. Success: 29 Radiant damage.
Call to Judgment. Nezekan chooses one creature he can see within 120 ft to become Judged, ending Judged on the previous creature.
Reactions
Answer the Deed — Reaction. When a creature within 30 ft damages Nezekan or an ally Nezekan can see, Nezekan moves up to 10 ft toward that creature without provoking Opportunity Attacks. If the creature is within his reach after the movement, Nezekan makes one Justice Blade attack against it.
Stand Before — Reaction. When an ally within 30 ft is hit by an attack, Nezekan moves up to 15 ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks to an unoccupied space within 5 ft of that ally. If Nezekan is a legal target of the triggering attack from the attacker's original position, Nezekan becomes the target and the same attack roll is resolved against his AC instead.
Lord Actions
Righteous Advance — Cost 1. Nezekan moves up to 25 ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks toward his Judged creature or toward a creature that damaged an ally since the end of Nezekan's last turn.
Righteous Reprisal — Cost 2. Nezekan makes one Justice Blade attack. If the target is Judged, a hit also prevents that target from taking Reactions until the start of Nezekan's next turn.
Final Sentence — Cost 3, Cooldown 2. One creature within 60 ft makes a DC 24 CHA save. Failure: 40 Radiant damage and Prone. Success: 20 Radiant damage.
Threshold
Justice Unbound — 50% Threshold (280 HP).

This triggers once. Nezekan may immediately choose a visible creature within 120 ft to become Judged and move up to 20 ft toward it without provoking Opportunity Attacks. His Judged damage rider becomes +15. Once per round thereafter, when the Judged creature damages a creature other than Nezekan, Nezekan may move up to 10 ft toward it without using a Reaction.

Opportunity Attack
Opportunity Attack. One Justice Blade.
