Traits & Signature
Light Incarnate.

Ariel's Light abilities have only their printed effects; her domain grants no hidden resistance, immunity, healing, or revelation.

Unclouded Sight.

Ariel's own Light effects never obscure her perception. This does not grant Truesight or sight through unrelated concealment.

Radiant Dominion — Signature.

Ariel may maintain up to 2 Radiant Zones. A Zone is a 15-ft-radius sphere centered on a point Ariel can see within 120 ft and lasts until Ariel dismisses it, replaces it beyond her maximum, or the encounter ends. At the start of her turn, Ariel may create one Zone or move one existing Zone up to 30 ft. Once on each of Ariel's turns when she deals Radiant damage to a creature, she may make that creature Illuminated until the start of her next turn. Illuminated is a keyword, not a Condition.

Actions
Sovereign Light. Ariel makes three Luminous Lance attacks.
Luminous Lance. +18 to hit, range 120 ft, one target. Hit: 26 Radiant damage, or 34 Radiant damage if the target is Illuminated.
Judgment of Light. One creature Ariel can see within 120 ft makes a DC 26 WIS save. Failure: 60 Radiant damage and Frightened until the end of its next turn. Success: 30 Radiant damage.
Seraphic Ward. One ally Ariel can see within 60 ft gains +3 AC until the start of Ariel's next turn.
Reactions
Intercession — Reaction. When Ariel or an ally she can see within 60 ft takes damage, reduce that damage by 25. After Light of Elysea triggers, reduce it by 35 instead.
Luminous Reversal — Reaction. When a creature Ariel can see within 120 ft makes an attack roll, Ariel forces that attack roll to be rerolled. The new result must be used.
Lord Actions
Lightstep — Cost 1. Ariel moves up to 20 ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks. She must begin or end this movement inside one of her Radiant Zones.
Piercing Revelation — Cost 2. One creature Ariel can see within 120 ft, or within one of her Radiant Zones, makes a DC 26 DEX save. Failure: 30 Radiant damage. Success: 15 Radiant damage. On a failure, the creature gains no benefit from invisibility or Half Cover against Ariel until the end of its next turn.
Crown of Light — Cost 3, Cooldown 2. One creature Ariel can see within 90 ft makes a DC 26 CHA save. Failure: 45 Radiant damage and Restrained until the end of its next turn. Success: 22 Radiant damage. Until the start of Ariel's next turn, Ariel gains +2 AC while inside one of her Radiant Zones.
Threshold
Light of Elysea — 50% Threshold (300 HP).

This triggers once. Ariel may immediately move up to 30 ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Her Radiant Zones become 20-ft-radius spheres for the rest of the encounter, and Intercession reduces damage by 35 instead of 25.

Opportunity Attack
Opportunity Attack. One Luminous Lance against the triggering creature if it is within 10 ft; never Sovereign Light.
