Traits & Signature
Mirrors — Signature.

Kaisinel may maintain up to 2 Mirrors. A Mirror is an intangible point in an unoccupied space Kaisinel can see within 60 ft. It occupies no space, blocks nothing, has no HP, and takes no turn. Kaisinel may measure the origin of Prism and his Lord Actions from himself or from a Mirror. The true Kaisinel remains mechanically identifiable.

Optimized Illusion.

A creature is in an optimized illusion position while it is within 10 ft of one of Kaisinel's Mirrors.

Actions
Prismatic Sequence. Kaisinel makes three Prism attacks.
Prism. +18 to hit, range 120 ft from Kaisinel or a Mirror, one target. Hit: 27 damage (14 Force + 13 Psychic), or 32 damage (16 Force + 16 Psychic) if the target is in an optimized illusion position.
Fractured Perception. One creature within 120 ft of Kaisinel or a Mirror makes a DC 26 INT save. Failure: 55 Psychic damage, cannot take Reactions, and has Disadvantage on its next attack roll before the end of its next turn. Success: 27 Psychic damage.
Hall of Mirrors. Kaisinel creates Mirrors up to his maximum or relocates any number of his existing Mirrors to unoccupied spaces he can see within 60 ft.
Reactions
False Target — Reaction. When an attack hits Kaisinel, he may consume one Mirror within 60 ft to force the triggering attack roll to be rerolled. The new result must be used.
Impossible Angle — Reaction. When Kaisinel is targeted by an attack or included in an area effect, he may teleport to the space of one Mirror within 60 ft before the attack or effect resolves. The Mirror moves to Kaisinel's former space. If Kaisinel is no longer a legal target or is outside the area, the attack or effect does not affect him.
Lord Actions
Reposition Image — Cost 1. Kaisinel swaps places with one Mirror within 60 ft. This teleportation does not provoke Opportunity Attacks.
Psychic Refraction — Cost 2. One creature within 90 ft of Kaisinel or a Mirror makes a DC 26 INT save. Failure: 28 Psychic damage, Speed -10 ft, and Disadvantage on its next attack roll until the end of its next turn. Success: 14 Psychic damage.
Palace of Illusions — Cost 3, Cooldown 2. Up to three creatures within 90 ft of Kaisinel or any Mirror make a DC 26 INT save. Failure: 40 Psychic damage, Speed is halved, and attack rolls have Disadvantage until the end of the creature's next turn. Success: 20 Psychic damage.
Threshold
Perfect Illusion — 50% Threshold (265 HP).

This triggers once. Kaisinel may immediately relocate both Mirrors. For the rest of the encounter, Mirror placement, swapping, and effect-origin range becomes 90 ft, and optimized illusion position extends to within 15 ft of a Mirror.

Opportunity Attack
Opportunity Attack. One Prism against the triggering creature if it is within 10 ft; never Prismatic Sequence.
