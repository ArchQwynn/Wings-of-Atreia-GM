SIX DRAGON LORDS

Dragon Lords

GM roster and encounter index. The six established Dragon Lords represent the highest tier of Balaur power in the campaign.

FIRST DRAGON LORD

Fregion

The first Dragon Lord in the established history of the Balaur. His rise marks the beginning of the Dragon Lord order.

DRAGON LORD

Meslamtaeda

One of the Six Dragon Lords and a power associated with the catastrophic military strength of the Balaur.

DRAGON LORD

Ereshkigal

One of the Six Dragon Lords. In the current campaign canon, Ereshkigal controls the Abyss.

DRAGON LORD

Beritra

One of the Six Dragon Lords and a major Balaur power whose deeper objectives belong in GM-facing lore.

DRAGON LORD

Tiamat

One of the Six Dragon Lords, associated with conquest, warfare, and Balaur dominion.

DRAGON LORD

Apsu

One of the Six Dragon Lords and part of the ancient Balaur power structure.

GM USE

Encounter scale

Dragon Lords should be treated as strategic campaign powers rather than ordinary monster encounters. Their armies, agents, territories, artifacts, schemes, and consequences can appear throughout the campaign long before a direct confrontation becomes appropriate.

The individual Lord records will hold the deeper secrets, relationships, current status, objectives, and statblocks when those entries are migrated into this repository.
