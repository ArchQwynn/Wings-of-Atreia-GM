LORDS ENCYCLOPEDIA · GM ONLY

The Lords of Atreia

Encyclopedic GM reference. This page records the established lore, identity, personality, portrayal, relationships, campaign use, and protected truths of the eighteen Lords. Combat mechanics remain in the separate statblock reference.

CANON FRAME

Empyrean Lords are extraordinary Daevas raised into Lordhood, not separately created gods. Their domains are affinities and identities, not cosmic monopolies; Aion alone is a true god. Lords are neither omnipotent nor omniscient. Fregion is the First Dragon Lord; he discovered Id, ascended Meslamtaeda, Ereshkigal, Beritra and Apsu, and later elevated Tiamat as the Sixth Dragon Lord.

SERAPHIM · LADY OF LIGHT

Ariel

StatusSeraphimDomainLight — revelation, guidance, hope, protection and moral clarity.PersonalityCompassionate, idealistic, luminous, diplomatic and resolute.

Portrayal

Portray Ariel as warm and regal without making her passive. She prefers persuasion, but acts decisively when lives are threatened. Her vulnerability is a tendency to insist that something remains salvageable after others have accepted that it is lost.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Yustiel is a close ally. Lumiel is the painful memory of a former friendship. Azphel is an ideological rival. Nezekan and Vaizel each challenge and reinforce different parts of Ariel's worldview.

Historical Context

Ariel supported Israphel's peace proposal sincerely and believed the negotiations were genuine. She later became a central figure in southern reconstruction after the Cataclysm.

SERAPHIM · LORD OF ILLUSION

Kaisinel

StatusSeraphimDomainIllusion — perception, interpretation, expectation, attention and belief.PersonalityRefined, cerebral, elusive, imaginative and observant.

Portrayal

Speak precisely and let Kaisinel notice what others overlook. He values creative solutions and understands that what people believe they see can shape their actions. He should not become a constant riddler.

Relationships & Campaign Use

His relationships with Ariel, Nezekan and Vaizel reflect shared Seraphim history. Israphel and Beritra provide useful thematic contrasts around manipulation and perception without making them identical.

Historical Context

Kaisinel sincerely supported Israphel's peace proposal and stood among the Seraphim who believed the negotiations were genuine.

SERAPHIM · LORD OF JUSTICE

Nezekan

StatusSeraphimDomainJustice — fairness, accountability, due process, proportional consequence and protection.PersonalityHonorable, disciplined, protective, principled and severe.

Portrayal

Measured and direct. He can be compassionate, but dislikes evasions that avoid responsibility. His concept of justice should remain fundamentally different from Ereshkigal's punitive legalism.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Triniel was his lover before the Tower and became a bitter enemy after the schism. Ereshkigal is his great philosophical opponent. Ariel's hope and Vaizel's freedom test the limits of his judgment.

Historical Context

Nezekan sincerely supported Israphel's peace proposal. His history makes him useful whenever a GM needs to explore the tension between law, compassion and responsibility.

SERAPHIM · LORD OF FREEDOM

Vaizel

StatusSeraphimDomainFreedom — meaningful choice rather than absence of consequence.PersonalityIndependent, adventurous, irreverent, courageous and restless.

Portrayal

Allow humor, curiosity and movement. Vaizel dislikes control for its own sake but is not allergic to responsibility. Freedom matters because choices have consequences.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Nezekan creates a natural law-versus-freedom tension. Marchutan's causality and Siel's continuity offer deeper contrasts. Apsu's later reinterpretation of Wind can become a powerful thematic parallel if it is discovered.

Historical Context

Vaizel sincerely supported the peace proposal and belongs to the Seraphim group that sought an end to the Millennium War stalemate.

SERAPHIM · LADY OF LIFE

Yustiel

StatusSeraphimDomainLife — healing, renewal, growth, adaptation, resilience and continuation.PersonalityWarm, compassionate, patient, resilient and nurturing.

Portrayal

Gentle does not mean weak. Yustiel understands grief and biological limits. She should not erase the meaning of death merely because she embodies Life.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Ariel is a close ally. Lumiel is a former friend. Triniel is a natural counterpart rather than a simple opposite. Siel's sacrifice remains an enduring lesson about life, loss and continuity.

Historical Context

Yustiel sincerely supported Israphel's peace proposal. Her philosophy makes her especially useful in stories about healing, survival, loss and the consequences of the Cataclysm.

SHEDIM · LORD OF SHADOW

Azphel

StatusShedimDomainShadow — shelter, vigilance, concealment and protection rather than moral darkness.PersonalityStern, pragmatic, commanding, suspicious and proud.

Portrayal

Controlled, disciplined and difficult to impress. His suspicion comes from survival and perceived historical betrayal; he is not automatically cruel or evil.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Ariel is his central ideological rival. Marchutan and Lumiel can share northern strategic and intellectual ground while disagreeing. Do not present Azphel as the unidentified Peace Conference attacker.

Historical Context

Azphel opposed Israphel's peace proposal. After the Cataclysm he became central to northern reconstruction and the development of the Asmodian side of the shattered world.

SHEDIM · LADY OF WISDOM

Lumiel

StatusShedimDomainWisdom — interpretation, context, uncertainty and understanding limits, not omniscience.PersonalityBrilliant, analytical, proud, scholarly and emotionally guarded.

Portrayal

Let Lumiel ask what evidence means rather than merely recite facts. Pride can make her harsh, especially around perceived Seraphim failure.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Ariel and Yustiel were once close friends, and that loss cuts deeply. Marchutan is a natural intellectual companion. Beritra's control of information represents a dangerous corruption of knowing.

Historical Context

Lumiel opposed the peace proposal and belongs to the Shedim group that rejected Israphel's approach.

Open Lumiel Statblock →
SHEDIM · LORD OF FATE

Marchutan

StatusShedimDomainFate — causality, consequence, responsibility and narrowing possibilities, not predestination.PersonalityPatient, solemn, calculating and composed.

Portrayal

Quiet and deliberate. Marchutan thinks in chains of cause and consequence. Never portray him as knowing a fixed future. Combat text may use “Discipline,” but his lore domain is Fate.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Vaizel's freedom provides a major philosophical contrast. Lumiel, Triniel, Nezekan and Siel each engage a different edge of responsibility, ending and possibility.

Historical Context

Marchutan opposed the peace proposal. His perspective is useful whenever a campaign explores whether choices create destiny or merely narrow future possibilities.

SHEDIM · LADY OF DEATH

Triniel

StatusShedimDomainDeath — endings, mortality, grief, release, remembrance and finality, not delight in needless killing.PersonalitySevere, composed, fearless, intimate with loss and emotionally guarded.

Portrayal

Triniel is comfortable naming what others avoid. Her severity can be merciful when prolonging suffering would be cruel.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Nezekan was her lover before the Tower and is now a bitter enemy. Yustiel provides a Life/Death dialogue rather than a simplistic opposition. Lumiel and Marchutan share northern history with her.

Historical Context

Triniel opposed Israphel's peace proposal. Her relationship with Nezekan provides one of the most personal fractures produced by the pre-Cataclysm schism.

Open Triniel Statblock →
SHEDIM · LORD OF DESTRUCTION

Zikel

StatusShedimDomainDestruction — breaking, demolition, structural failure, upheaval and decisive violence.PersonalityAggressive, disciplined, uncompromising, martial and purposeful.

Portrayal

Do not make Zikel a mindless berserker. He asks what must be broken and whether destruction is justified. His restraint before Deltras's first strike is important historical evidence.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Marchutan, Lumiel and Triniel provide strategic and ideological context. Ariel and Yustiel can expose his suspicion of peace without making him incapable of reason.

Historical Context

Zikel opposed the peace proposal. During the later rediscovery of the Abyss, Deltras struck him first; Zikel killed Deltras in retaliation. The event became a foundational divide between Elyos and Asmodian historical memory.

TOWER GUARDIAN · LORD OF SPACE

Israphel

StatusTower Guardian · MissingDomainSpace — relationship, boundary, proximity, access, isolation and inside/outside.PersonalityBrilliant, devious, ambitious, manipulative and charismatic.

Portrayal

Historical Israphel was persuasive enough that sincere allies supported him. Only he knew the peace proposal was a trap. His deception must not erase the fact that he later fought beside Siel to defend the Tower.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Siel was his fellow Tower Guardian. Beritra was the target of his false alliance offer. The would-be assassin at the conference remains unidentified.

Protected Truth

Israphel intended to lure the Dragon Lords inside the Great Barrier without their legions and destroy them. His anti-Aion bargain with Beritra was false. He disappeared after the Tower's destruction; his fate remains unresolved unless a campaign-specific decision is made in deliberately open space.

TOWER GUARDIAN · LADY OF TIME

Siel

StatusTower Guardian · Permanently DeadDomainTime — continuity, transformation, memory and the meaning change gives to choice.PersonalitySerene, compassionate, resolute, reflective and self-sacrificing.

Portrayal

When portraying Siel historically, do not reduce every scene to foreshadowing her martyrdom. She lived, disagreed, chose and was persuaded before making her final sacrifice.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Israphel was her fellow Tower Guardian. Her final sacrifice prevented complete annihilation and ended ancient inherent Daeva resurrection.

Permanent Canon

Siel is permanently dead. There is no hidden survival, resurrection quest or reincarnation baseline. Echoes, memories and temporal impressions are not Siel returned.

DRAGON LORD · LORD OF FIRE

Fregion

StatusDragon Lord · First Dragon LordDomainFire — transformative destruction, supremacy and world-shaping force.PersonalityImperial, immensely powerful, commanding, destructive and loyal to the order of the Six on his terms.

Portrayal

Fregion should feel foundational to Balaur history, not merely like a large combatant. Aion ascended him; he discovered Id; he created the Original Dragon Lords and later Tiamat.

Relationships & Campaign Use

He deliberately destroyed the Tower to break the Great Barrier. He later created the Theobomos fire scar. Do not invent a default present location beyond what canon fixes.

Historical Context

Fregion was Aion's chosen first Dragon Lord while the Drakans were still genuine protectors. His discovery of Id enabled him to reproduce the scale of transformation Aion had granted him and ascend the other Original Lords.

DRAGON LORD · RAGE & DESTRUCTION

Meslamtaeda

StatusDragon LordDomainRage and Destruction — direct, personal annihilation and overwhelming violence.PersonalityViolent, proud, confrontational, supremacist and confident.

Portrayal

Rage need not mean constant shouting. It can be crushing certainty that resistance deserves annihilation. Destruction is direct and personal.

Relationships & Campaign Use

He respects Fregion's supremacy and underestimates Ereshkigal. His contempt should never be confused with stupidity; he is an apex threat.

Historical Context

Meslamtaeda's Daeva Slaughter became the defining demonstration of individual Dragon Lord power. He personally killed more Daevas than any other Dragon Lord and forced the united Empyreans to combine merely to make him retreat.

DRAGON LORD · ICE & JUSTICE

Ereshkigal

StatusDragon Lord · HibernatingDomainJustice — verdict, consequence, deterrence and punishment; Ice — containment, preservation, brittleness and precise shattering.PersonalityImperious, elegant, disciplined, punitive, proud and destructive.

Portrayal

The quieter Ereshkigal becomes, the worse the situation may be. She takes pleasure in destructive Ice, creating the danger of a judge who enjoys punishment.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Only Fregion clearly surpasses her; she is approximately Meslamtaeda's peer. Tiamat, Beritra and others underestimate her.

Present Age

Ereshkigal is alive and hibernating. She later froze a vast part of Morheim and restored central Balaur order before entering hibernation. Elyos and Asmodians do not know her location; her sovereignty is the strategic order she established, not visible daily administration.

DRAGON LORD · DARKNESS

Beritra

StatusDragon LordDomainDarkness — uncertainty, selective truth, hidden motives, controlled information and manufactured context.PersonalityIntelligent, secretive, charismatic, manipulative, patient and ambitious.

Portrayal

Do not make Beritra constantly whisper or sneer. He can be cultured, funny and genuinely attentive because information is easiest to collect when others want to speak.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Apsu was his one genuine brother-like Dragon Lord bond. He hates Tiamat after her seizure of his forces and attack on Apsu. He believed Israphel's false bargain and was the assassination target, not its architect.

Historical Context

Beritra was deceived by Israphel's false promise that Empyreans and Dragon Lords would make peace, ascend together and kill Aion. An unidentified attacker later attempted to assassinate him; his lieutenant intercepted the fatal strike and died. The attacker's identity remains unresolved.

DRAGON LORD · EARTH & DEATH

Tiamat

StatusDragon Lord · SixthDomainEarth — foundations and structures; Death — irreversible consequence and what becomes possible after something ends.PersonalityAmbitious, opportunistic, destructive, strategic and expansionist.

Portrayal

Tiamat rarely announces the true attack. She looks for the supporting structure — road, commander, institution or fortress foundation — and breaks what lets the rest stand.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Fregion elevated her as the Sixth Dragon Lord. She seized many of Beritra's forces during his absence, defeated Apsu and outranked him, and remains at war with Beritra. She plans eventually to challenge Ereshkigal and dangerously underestimates her.

Historical Context

Tiamat's power is political and strategic as well as destructive. Her conflict with Beritra and her treatment of Apsu are important tools for Dragon Lord intrigue.

DRAGON LORD · WIND

Apsu

StatusDragon Lord · Present, Weakened & HiddenDomainWind — originally freedom to go where he wished regardless of others; later freedom from domination itself.PersonalityProud and culpable in his past; later reflective, ruthless, self-condemning and strategically useful.

Portrayal

Present Apsu is not a saint and does not ask for forgiveness. He recognizes that the Balaur order and every Dragon Lord, including himself, must ultimately end. If the PCs encounter him, his willingness to cooperate is genuine, but the meeting is not a mandatory future event.

Relationships & Campaign Use

Tiamat defeated but did not kill him. He is alive, severely weakened and hidden. Beritra once loved him like a brother; Apsu no longer allows that affection to exempt Beritra from his judgment.

Protected GM Secret

Ordinary Daevas do not know Apsu survived. If a campaign eventually brings him into play, his later anti-Balaur conclusion and genuine willingness to cooperate are part of synchronized Lord canon, but the timing and circumstances of any meeting are not mandatory.

LORD-CENTERED CAMPAIGN GUIDANCE

A useful escalation path is legacy and rumors → subordinates → agents → direct consequences → audience or confrontation → possible Lord-scale resolution. This is guidance, not a mandatory ladder. Existing Lord relationships should shape requests, rivalries and strategy; strong disagreement does not automatically mean constant lethal conflict. Do not casually invent major new Lord romances, bloodlines, secret identities or equivalent canon-changing relationships.
