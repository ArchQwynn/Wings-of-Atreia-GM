Bloodwing

Bloodwing
Bloodwing are aerial predators.
Ecology & Behavior. Across arid wilderness, ancient ruins, broken canyons, and sparse scrub as well as snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland, Bloodwing spend much of their lives moving between high perches and open feeding grounds. Their calls carry farther than their bodies are usually visible, making a colony or nesting pair easier to hear than to approach. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold around nests, repeatedly passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Ancient Bloodwing, Merciless Ancient Bloodwing, and Shadow Bloodwing are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols as well as asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols often use the behavior of Bloodwing as an informal warning system: sudden circling, mass flight, or silence can reveal predators, approaching troops, or violent Aetheric weather before it is obvious from the ground.
Locations. Theobomos, Morheim
Variants and Named Examples. Ancient Bloodwing; Merciless Ancient Bloodwing; Shadow Bloodwing; Nightstalker
