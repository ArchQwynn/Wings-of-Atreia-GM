Tigric
Tigric are feline humanoid warriors. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and organization vary by group.
Ecology & Behavior. Across dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as arid wilderness, ancient
ruins, broken canyons, and sparse scrub, Tigric live in organized social groups whose size changes with circumstance.
Small settlements can be practical and family-centered, while threatened communities rapidly become more militarized,
posting sentries and concentrating supplies behind defensible positions. Competition between leaders can produce
raiding and territorial violence, yet the same groups are capable of truces, hostage exchanges, hired service, trade, and
temporary alliances when survival makes cooperation more valuable than pride. Forms such as Akeras Stripeback, and
Aeution Firemane are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader
family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Daevas encounter Tigric not only as enemies but as guides, laborers, traders, rivals, refugees, prisoners,
mercenaries, and local powers. The reaction of a particular group depends on its recent history with Elyos, Asmodians,
Balaur, and neighboring peoples.
Locations. Eltnen, Heiron, Theobomos
Variants and Named Examples. Akeras Stripeback; Aeution Firemane
