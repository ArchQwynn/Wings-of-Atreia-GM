Crasaur
Crasaurs are heavy animals capable of powerful physical impacts. Ice Crasaurs live around Altgard Ice Lake and use
thrashing and forceful knockback.
Ecology & Behavior. Crasaur are heavy-bodied animals that claim broad feeding ranges through cold uplands, ice-
rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. Their size lets them push through brush, break crusted ground, and reach
food that smaller grazers or browsers cannot easily exploit. Most would rather warn an intruder than pursue it, but once
committed they use weight, horns, shell, or sheer momentum to clear space around themselves. The Ice Crasaur is
commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Crasaur family.
Life in Atreia. Large Crasaur are economically significant wherever they occur. Asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols
prize hide, horn, shell, bone, meat, or sinew, but mature animals are rarely worth provoking without preparation.
Locations. Altgard
