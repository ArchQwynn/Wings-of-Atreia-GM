Mamut
Mamuts are enormous tusked creatures. Adults in Geroch Caldera hunt with vicious tusks.
Ecology & Behavior. The species is built for endurance rather than stealth. In dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and
sun-baked ruins, adults move between reliable feeding grounds and water, often returning to the same paths until those
routes become obvious tracks through the landscape. Most would rather warn an intruder than pursue it, but once
committed they use weight, horns, shell, or sheer momentum to clear space around themselves. The Moomba the
Earthshaker is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Mamut family.
Life in Atreia. Large Mamut are economically significant wherever they occur. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols prize
hide, horn, shell, bone, meat, or sinew, but mature animals are rarely worth provoking without preparation.
