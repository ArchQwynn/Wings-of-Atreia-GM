Malekor
Malekor are magical mineral creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Unlike living fauna, Malekor do not need a stable food web. Their presence in dense Balaurean
wilderness, Aether-rich growth, cliffs, and warfront routes as well as Balaurean wilds shaped by war, immense ruins,
strange vegetation, and powerful Aether usually points to a creator, a forgotten defense, a damaged Aetheric process, or
a site whose magic continues to function long after its builders disappeared. Damage does not frighten them in the
manner of animals. A construct generally stops because its structure or animating force fails, not because pain
persuades it to retreat.
Life in Atreia. Balaurean expeditions, fortress patrols, and frontier daevas treat intact Malekor as both threat and
archaeological evidence. Salvagers value fragments, cores, runes, and unusual stonework, while scholars are more
interested in what the construct's behavior reveals about the site that produced it.
Locations. Inggison, Gelkmaros, Greater Balaurea
