Ksellid
Ksellids are heavily shelled beasts found around lakes and deserts. Their young can mature into larger stoneshell forms,
and adult shells are tough enough to be harvested.
Ecology & Behavior. Across cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads as well as snowfields, high
valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland, Ksellid function as major landscape animals. They open trails through dense
growth, churn soil around watering places, and leave feeding grounds that are quickly used by smaller creatures. Most
would rather warn an intruder than pursue it, but once committed they use weight, horns, shell, or sheer momentum to
clear space around themselves. Forms such as Bearded Ksellid, Lake Ksellid, and Star Metal Ksellid are commonly
interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated
creatures.
Life in Atreia. Communities near Ksellid habitat build fences and storage areas with the animal's strength in mind. A
single frightened adult can damage crops or structures without behaving as a deliberate attacker.
Locations. Altgard, Morheim
Variants and Named Examples. Bearded Ksellid; Lake Ksellid; Star Metal Ksellid; Stonescale Kselliki (form/juvenile);
Andre; Speedy
