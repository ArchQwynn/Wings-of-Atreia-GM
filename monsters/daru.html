Daru
Daru range from meek forest animals to tougher regional forms. Whitefoot Daru are described as meek inhabitants of
Munihele Forest, while Morheim Daru can fight with horns and powerful forefeet and may compete with predators such
as Worgs.
Ecology & Behavior. The species thrives along the margins of wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as
well as cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement country, where vegetation, loose stone, roots, and
shallow burrows provide many places to hide. Their alarm behavior can warn other nearby animals, and experienced
hunters sometimes read sudden silence or flight as a sign that something larger is approaching. Forms such as Half-
Moon Daru, Whitefoot Daru, and Quick Daru are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized
expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. The species appears in local stories precisely because it is familiar. Children learn to recognize it, hunters
know which parts are useful, and patrols understand when its unusual behavior signals a greater disturbance in the
region.
Locations. Poeta, Ishalgen
Variants and Named Examples. Half-Moon Daru; Whitefoot Daru; Quick Daru; Savvy Daru; Hammerhoof
