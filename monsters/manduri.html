Manduri
Manduri form settled communities in Eltnen, including Manduri Village and nearby food stores. Source records show
gatherers, hunters, fighters, and looters. Their communities contain distinct practical roles.
Ecology & Behavior. Manduri communities are shaped less by a single universal culture than by the pressures of dry
grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins. Bands, villages, camps, and war parties develop around
dependable food, defensible ground, trade routes, and the authority of leaders who can keep the group supplied. Tool
use and local knowledge let them exploit resources that nearby beasts cannot, and even a modest camp tends to
contain caches, work spaces, paths, and signs of repeated occupation.
Life in Atreia. Relations with Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols vary from open hostility to cautious exchange. Food,
metalwork, medicines, information, and safe passage can all become bargaining points, while old raids or territorial
claims can turn a tense meeting violent with little warning.
