Virago
Viragos are humanoid avian creatures with human-like bodies and birdlike wings and legs. Kuriullu Mountain forms
display ornate colored wings and can behave as scavengers or outlaws.
Ecology & Behavior. Virago society is built around cooperation, reputation, and access to resources. In dry grasslands,
badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland, the
ability to secure food, tools, shelter, and safe passage matters as much as personal strength, and successful leaders are
usually those who can provide all four. Their warriors rarely operate in isolation. Scouts, laborers, hunters, healers, and
noncombatants support every armed group, so a fortified camp is as much a community under pressure as it is a military
target. Forms such as Virago Outlaw, Sentinel, and Scavenger are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or
specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Daevas encounter Virago not only as enemies but as guides, laborers, traders, rivals, refugees, prisoners,
mercenaries, and local powers. The reaction of a particular group depends on its recent history with Elyos, Asmodians,
Balaur, and neighboring peoples.
