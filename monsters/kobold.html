Kobold

Source-locked complete migration.

Kobolds are tribal sapiens of Elysea whose communities are closely tied to Krall tribes. Their society is shamanistic, their religious imagery anthropomorphizes animals and creatures, and many Kobolds labor in Krall mining operations rather than serving primarily as frontline warriors.

Ecology & Behavior. Across woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and settled roads, Kobold live in organized social groups whose size changes with circumstance. Small settlements can be practical and family-centered, while threatened communities rapidly become more militarized, posting sentries and concentrating supplies behind defensible positions. Movement between settlements carries stories, goods, techniques, and grudges, giving even isolated groups a wider awareness of regional events than outsiders often expect. Forms such as Mamaki subgroup, Brawler, and Bruiser are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. The wider wars of Atreia constantly intrude on Kobold life. Armies requisition food, monsters displace settlements, and ruined roads create opportunities for smuggling and salvage; communities respond by choosing whichever combination of resistance, neutrality, or accommodation keeps them alive.

Locations. Verteron

Variants and Named Examples. Mamaki subgroup; Brawler; Bruiser; Cook; Worker; Butcher; Flagbearer; Peon; Tracker; Vigilante

RANK FEATURES

Rank Features

Base

Scrappy Attack. This is the family’s characteristic attack pattern and remains inside the normal action damage budget.

Veteran+

Mob Pressure. Once per turn, gain +2 to one attack roll when a relevant ally is within 5 ft of the target or when the creature began the turn unseen by that target. This bonus does not stack.

Elite+

Dirty Trick. Cooldown 2. Make one signature Weapon Strike. On a hit, deal about 125-150% of the normal Band/Rank damage.

Boss+

Pack Command. Cooldown 2. Choose up to two allied creatures of the same family within 30 ft that can hear the Boss. Each may move up to 10 ft or stand from Prone without spending its Action.
