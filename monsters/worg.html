Worg

Source-locked complete migration.

Worgs are pack predators found across Atreia. Their behavior changes with circumstance: starving Verteron packs have become desperate around ruined farms, while named individuals can lead groups of lesser Worgs.

Ecology & Behavior. Worgs are social pack predators that adapt quickly to the instability of Atreia. Packs use scent, calls, and coordinated movement to test prey before committing to an attack, with experienced adults steering younger animals away from unnecessary injury. In healthy wilderness they help control grazing animals and scavengers, but famine, winter, or war can push packs toward farms, roads, and battlefields where food is concentrated and human defenses are inconsistent.

Life in Atreia. Both Elyos and Asmodians understand that a Worg problem is often a symptom of a larger disruption. Starving packs near ruined farms indicate displaced prey or abandoned land; unusually bold animals may have learned to associate soldiers with carrion. Some communities tame individual Worgs as guards or hunting animals, proving that their aggression is not mindless. Named pack leaders can become local legends because removing one may scatter a pack—or simply make room for an even more dangerous successor.

Locations. Verteron, Morheim, Beluslan

Variants and Named Examples. Gray Worg; Fierce Gray Worg; Starving Worg; Violent Worg; Ruthless Worg; Angry Worg. States/Templates: Tamed Worg; Undead Worg. Deferred named instance example: Wounded Scar.

RANK FEATURES

Rank Features

Base

Pack Hunter. Once per turn, gain +2 to one attack roll when a relevant ally is within 5 ft of the target or when the creature began the turn unseen by that target. This bonus does not stack.

Veteran+

Pounce. Once per turn, after moving at least 15 ft toward a hostile creature and hitting it, force a STR save. On a failure, the target becomes Prone.

Elite+

Savage Mauling. Cooldown 2. Make one signature attack. On a hit, deal about 125–150% of the normal Band/Rank damage.

Boss+

Alpha's Howl. Cooldown 2. Choose up to two allied creatures of the same family within 30 ft that can hear the Boss. Each may move up to 10 ft or stand from Prone without spending its Action.
