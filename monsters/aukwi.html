Aukwi
Aukwi are small birds recorded in Morheim and Brusthonin.
Ecology & Behavior. The species ranges widely through snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland as well as remote woodland, damp lowlands, coastward country, and abandoned tracts, usually maintaining hunting territories centered on shelter and dependable prey. Individuals become most visible at dawn, dusk, or after disturbance drives prey into the open. They are not mindless killers: a well-fed animal normally avoids a costly fight, while starving, wounded, or territorial individuals can become far more dangerous. The Dukar is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Aukwi family.
Life in Atreia. Hunters read Aukwi sign carefully when moving through the wild. Fresh kills, repeated tracks, and territorial marks can alter a patrol route, while old dens sometimes become useful shelters after the animals move on.
Locations. Morheim, Brusthonin
