Ampha

Small Beast · Durability: Light · Combat Identity: Poisonous creature

Ecology & Behavior

Ampha can be highly dangerous predators. The Poisonsac Ampha of Gribade Canyon is explicitly poisonous and described as refusing to release prey once it has sunk in its teeth. The species flourishes in cluttered habitats across cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement country as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. Chitin, spines, venom, or webbing allow even small individuals to defend feeding sites that would otherwise be taken by larger predators. Their life cycle tends to produce sudden local population booms when food and warmth are plentiful, followed by sharp declines when prey becomes scarce.

Life in Atreia

Settlements near Ampha habitat clear brush, burn abandoned nests, and keep paths open around wells and storehouses. Skilled hunters, however, sometimes preserve remote colonies because their presence suppresses other vermin.
