Ampha

Source-locked complete migration.

Ampha can be highly dangerous predators. The Poisonsac Ampha of Gribade Canyon is explicitly poisonous and described as refusing to release prey once it has sunk in its teeth.

Ecology & Behavior. The species flourishes in cluttered habitats across cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement country as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. Chitin, spines, venom, or webbing allow even small individuals to defend feeding sites that would otherwise be taken by larger predators. Their life cycle tends to produce sudden local population booms when food and warmth are plentiful, followed by sharp declines when prey becomes scarce. Forms such as Thorned Ampha, and Poisonsac Ampha are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Settlements near Ampha habitat clear brush, burn abandoned nests, and keep paths open around wells and storehouses. Skilled hunters, however, sometimes preserve remote colonies because their presence suppresses other vermin.

Locations. Ishalgen, Altgard

Variants and Named Examples. Thorned Ampha; Poisonsac Ampha

Venom Burst. Cooldown 2. Make one signature Bite attack. On a hit, deal about 125-150% of the normal Band/Rank damage; the target then makes a CON save and is Poisoned until the end of its next turn on a failure. Rank Stage Feature

Boss+

Toxic Presence. Cooldown 2. Chosen hostile creatures within 10 ft make a CON save. On a failure, a creature is Poisoned until the end of its next turn. This feature deals no additional damage.
