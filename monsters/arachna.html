Arachna
Arachna are dangerous arachnid wildlife found in multiple environments. Poison Arachna inhabit Mahindel Swamp and
possess deadly poison, while other regional forms differ in color and hunting adaptations.
Ecology & Behavior. Arachna are low-slung hunters and scavengers well suited to the sheltered ground of wooded
valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads.
They favor cracks, roots, burrows, ruins, and dense vegetation where a small body can remain unseen until prey is
close. Their life cycle tends to produce sudden local population booms when food and warmth are plentiful, followed by
sharp declines when prey becomes scarce. Forms such as Tiny Arachna, Tiny Cave Arachna, and Venomous Arachna
are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than
entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Settlements near Arachna habitat clear brush, burn abandoned nests, and keep paths open around wells
and storehouses. Skilled hunters, however, sometimes preserve remote colonies because their presence suppresses
other vermin.
Locations. Poeta, Verteron, Heiron, Altgard
Variants and Named Examples. Tiny Arachna; Tiny Cave Arachna; Venomous Arachna; Nimble Arachna; Poison
Arachna; Stinging Arachna; Arachnaling — juvenile/developmental Arachna form
