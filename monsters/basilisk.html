Basilisk
Basilisk are reptilian creatures noted for controlling nearby foes.
Ecology & Behavior. Basilisk are ectothermic-looking wilderness animals most active where exposed stone and
sheltered ground alternate across dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins. They spend long periods
conserving energy before moving decisively to feed, defend territory, or seek water. Sun-warmed ruins, cliff faces, and
road embankments make especially attractive resting places, which is why travelers can encounter them in places that
otherwise appear empty. The Snaggletooth is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of
the broader Basilisk family.
Life in Atreia. The species appears in local stories precisely because it is familiar. Children learn to recognize it, hunters
know which parts are useful, and patrols understand when its unusual behavior signals a greater disturbance in the
region.
