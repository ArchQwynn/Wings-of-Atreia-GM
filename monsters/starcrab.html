Starcrab
Starcrabs are crustaceans found in swamps and other wet habitats. They are harvested for meat and carapaces, but
pointed and sharp-horned forms can defend themselves aggressively with piercing attacks.
Ecology & Behavior. Starcrab occupy wet ground, shorelines, pools, and water-cut hollows within woodlands, river
country, ruined approaches, and settled roads. Much of their activity follows moisture: they forage most confidently
where mud, reeds, submerged stone, or shallow water gives them cover. Their feeding helps clear carrion and soft
organic debris from waterways, though dense populations can also strip spawning beds and shallow pools of smaller life.
The Sharphorn Starcrab is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader
Starcrab family.
Life in Atreia. Communities near Starcrab habitat build fences and storage areas with the animal's strength in mind. A
single frightened adult can damage crops or structures without behaving as a deliberate attacker.
Locations. Verteron
