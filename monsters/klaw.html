Klaw
Klaws form concentrations large enough for whole habitats to be recognized. Morheim populations include peons,
sentinels, patrols, spies, and spawnmasters, indicating organized colonies rather than purely solitary creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Klaw are low-slung hunters and scavengers well suited to the sheltered ground of dry grasslands,
badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as floating islands, exposed ledges, fortress approaches, and unstable
Aether currents. They favor cracks, roots, burrows, ruins, and dense vegetation where a small body can remain unseen
until prey is close. They are important consumers of insects, vermin, and carrion, but their proximity to camps and
storehouses makes them a recurring nuisance to travelers. Forms such as Klaw Worker, Gatherer, and Soldier are
commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely
unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Settlements near Klaw habitat clear brush, burn abandoned nests, and keep paths open around wells and
storehouses. Skilled hunters, however, sometimes preserve remote colonies because their presence suppresses other
vermin.
Locations. Eltnen, Heiron, Morheim, Reshanta
Variants and Named Examples. Klaw Worker; Gatherer; Soldier; Sentry; Spawner; Queen Klawtaka; Queen Klawterun
