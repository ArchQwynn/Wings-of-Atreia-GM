Gurriki
Gurriki are sapient skirmishers. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and organization vary by group.
Ecology & Behavior. Gurriki society is built around cooperation, reputation, and access to resources. In Balaurean wilds
shaped by war, immense ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether, the ability to secure food, tools, shelter, and
safe passage matters as much as personal strength, and successful leaders are usually those who can provide all four.
Movement between settlements carries stories, goods, techniques, and grudges, giving even isolated groups a wider
awareness of regional events than outsiders often expect.
Life in Atreia. Relations with Balaurean expeditions, fortress patrols, and frontier Daevas vary from open hostility to
cautious exchange. Food, metalwork, medicines, information, and safe passage can all become bargaining points, while
old raids or territorial claims can turn a tense meeting violent with little warning.
