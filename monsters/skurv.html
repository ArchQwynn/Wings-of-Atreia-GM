Skurv
Skurv are organized small folk seen farming, gathering herbs, patrolling settlements, trading, and forming named tribes
such as the MuMu and BuBu. Their agricultural work is especially important to Mau society, where the relationship is
described as symbiotic: Mau provide protection and organization while Skurv provide crops and labor.
Ecology & Behavior. Skurv are small organized folk whose settlements revolve around cultivation, gathering, food
storage, and tightly coordinated labor. Their fields and herb plots can support populations much larger than their stature
suggests, and their communities are full of paths, drying racks, baskets, irrigation ditches, and carefully guarded stores.
Where they live beside Mau groups, the relationship can become genuinely interdependent: Skurv agricultural labor
sustains the settlement while Mau warriors provide protection and access to wider territory.
Life in Atreia. Asmodian Daevas who meet Skurv in Ishalgen or Altgard may encounter farmers defending crops,
traders carrying herbs, scouts reporting threats, or workers caught between rival Mau leaders. Their small size and non-
Daeva status do not make them politically irrelevant. A damaged harvest can alter local Mau behavior, drive theft or
migration, and change which roads are safe long before a formal military force notices the problem.
Locations. Ishalgen, Altgard
