Gumi
Gumi are small reptilian creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Gumi are quick, alert animals common to sheltered pockets of arid wilderness, ancient ruins,
broken canyons, and sparse scrub as well as remote woodland, damp lowlands, coastward country, and abandoned
tracts. They survive less by fighting than by noticing danger early, using cover and bursts of speed to escape larger
hunters. Individuals frequently freeze before fleeing, relying on coloration and stillness until the last safe moment. The
Striped Gumi is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Gumi family.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols as well as asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols know Gumi as part
of the ordinary living landscape rather than as a monster that must always be fought. Hunters, herders, and scouts learn
its tracks and calls because they reveal water, predators, weather shifts, and the recent passage of larger creatures.
Locations. Theobomos, Altgard, Brusthonin
Variants and Named Examples. Striped Gumi
