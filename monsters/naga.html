Naga
Naga are sapient serpentine combatants. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and organization vary by group.
Ecology & Behavior. Across floating islands, exposed ledges, fortress approaches, and unstable Aether currents as
well as Balaurean wilds shaped by war, immense ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether, Naga live in organized
social groups whose size changes with circumstance. Small settlements can be practical and family-centered, while
threatened communities rapidly become more militarized, posting sentries and concentrating supplies behind defensible
positions. Tool use and local knowledge let them exploit resources that nearby beasts cannot, and even a modest camp
tends to contain caches, work spaces, paths, and signs of repeated occupation. Forms such as Naga (female forms),
Nagarant (male forms), and Kuninasha. Martial and magical roles are tendencies, not universal sex-based mechanics.
are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than
entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. The wider wars of Atreia constantly intrude on Naga life. Armies requisition food, monsters displace
settlements, and ruined roads create opportunities for smuggling and salvage; communities respond by choosing
whichever combination of resistance, neutrality, or accommodation keeps them alive.
Locations. Reshanta, Inggison, Gelkmaros, Silentera Canyon, Greater Balaurea
Variants and Named Examples. Naga (female forms); Nagarant (male forms); Kuninasha. Martial and magical roles are
tendencies, not universal sex-based mechanics.
