Cliona
Cliona are creatures adapted to both flight and swimming.
Ecology & Behavior. Cliona favor elevated nesting or resting sites overlooking wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old
forest paths. They use height as protection and as a way to survey feeding grounds, descending only when prey, carrion,
water, or nesting material draws them down. Flocks and loose colonies communicate through sharp calls and rapid
changes in flight, allowing danger to spread through a nesting ground almost instantly.
Life in Atreia. Because Daevas themselves travel through the air, Cliona are more than background wildlife. Elyos
hunters, farmers, and patrols learn the locations of major roosts and avoid crossing them at low altitude, especially
during nesting season.
