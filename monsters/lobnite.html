Lobnite
Lobnite are tentacled creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. The species thrives along the wettest parts of woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and
settled roads as well as remote woodland, damp lowlands, coastward country, and abandoned tracts, where it feeds
among silt, reeds, small animals, carrion, and whatever organic matter collects at the water's edge. Their feeding helps
clear carrion and soft organic debris from waterways, though dense populations can also strip spawning beds and
shallow pools of smaller life. Forms such as Spiral Lobnite, and Tentacled Lobnite are commonly interpreted as regional,
age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. The species often brings wilderness directly into settled life: nets, fish traps, irrigation channels, and ferry
landings can all attract Lobnite. Communities usually tolerate them until feeding damage or attacks become frequent.
Locations. Verteron, Altgard, Beluslan, Brusthonin
Variants and Named Examples. Spiral Lobnite; Tentacled Lobnite
