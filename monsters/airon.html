Airon

Small Beast · Durability: Light · Combat Identity: Aerial birdlike predator

Ecology & Behavior

Airon favor elevated nesting or resting sites overlooking woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and settled roads as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. They use height as protection and as a way to survey feeding grounds, descending only when prey, carrion, water, or nesting material draws them down. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold around nests, repeatedly passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Brownbeak Airon, Ice Airon, and Tirratirra are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family.

Life in Atreia

Because Daevas themselves travel through the air, Airon are more than background wildlife. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols as well as Asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols learn the locations of major roosts and avoid crossing them at low altitude, especially during nesting season.

Rank Features

Base — Peck/Talon. This establishes the family’s characteristic combat behavior and does not add another full-damage action.

Veteran+ — Swoop. Once per turn while airborne, move up to 10 ft immediately before or after the creature’s attack. This movement grants no additional attack.

Elite+ — Diving Strike. Cooldown 2. While airborne, make one signature Talon attack. On a hit, deal about 125–150% of the normal Band/Rank damage.

Boss+ — Screech. Cooldown 2. This is the family’s Boss-rank expression and does not grant an extra full-damage turn.

Actions

Talons (Slashing; reach 5 ft). Use the selected Band/Rank ATK against AC; on a hit, deal the selected normal DMG.

Boss Action

Wingbeat Shift. Move up to half its Fly Speed. If it is grounded or cannot fly, it may instead move up to half its land Speed. This is ordinary movement and can provoke Opportunity Attacks.
