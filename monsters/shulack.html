Shulack
Shulacks are Balaurean sapiens whose scattered villages are normally led by an elder. Their reputation for piracy and
grave-robbing hides a broader society that includes ordinary villagers, builders, craftsmen, hired workers, refugees, and
groups coerced into Balaur service.
Ecology & Behavior. Shulack settlements are compact communities built around salvage, craft, trade, and an
extraordinary ability to make use of whatever stronger powers leave behind. Their reputation for piracy and grave-
robbing grows from the same survival instinct that makes them effective builders, repairers, traders, and hired workers. A
village led by an elder may contain sailors, scavengers, cooks, smiths, merchants, children, refugees, and armed
lookouts all within the same crowded space.
Life in Atreia. In Balaurea, Shulacks survive by reading the balance of power faster than almost anyone else. They may
work for Balaur when refusal would mean destruction, sell supplies to Daevas when profit and safety align, or abandon a
settlement entirely when a warfront shifts. This flexibility makes them unreliable allies but excellent sources of rumor,
contraband, maps, and salvaged equipment. Treating every Shulack as a pirate guarantees enemies; treating every
Shulack as harmless is equally foolish.
