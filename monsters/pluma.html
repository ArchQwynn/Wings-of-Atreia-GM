Pluma

Source-locked complete migration.

Plumas are colorful birdlike wildlife distributed across forests, swamps, fortresses, and frozen lakes. Their many regional forms vary strongly in local coloration and habitat.

Ecology & Behavior. The species is most at home where open air meets broken terrain. In woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and settled roads as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads, Pluma circle above clearings and ridges, using vertical space to escape larger ground predators and to watch for disturbances below. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold around nests, repeatedly passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Glade Pluma, Leaf Pluma, and Contaminated Pluma are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Feathers, membranes, eggshell, or shed hornlike material from Pluma sometimes enter local craft traditions, but taking them from an active nesting site is far more dangerous than collecting naturally discarded material.

Locations. Verteron, Altgard

Variants and Named Examples. Glade Pluma; Leaf Pluma; Contaminated Pluma; Crested Pluma; Grove Pluma; Swamp Pluma; Ice Lake Pluma; Gossiper Jin
