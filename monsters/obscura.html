Obscura
Obscura are supernatural ranged attackers.
Ecology & Behavior. The presence of Obscura is usually felt before it is understood. In dry grasslands, badlands,
springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as tundra, ruined strongholds, frozen valleys, and windswept passes, witnesses
report altered sounds, misleading movement, unusual lights, or an oppressive sense of being observed before the
creature finally reveals itself. Some can be driven away when the condition sustaining them changes; others simply
retreat and return later. This uncertainty is why old stories about them tend to preserve practical taboos even when the
explanations differ from village to village. Forms such as Shadow Obscura, Blazing Obscura, and Raging Obscura are
commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely
unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Encounters with Obscura leave a cultural mark disproportionate to their numbers. A single dangerous site
can alter a road, create a local taboo, or inspire a shrine intended less to worship the creature than to keep travelers
away from it.
Locations. Eltnen, Beluslan
Variants and Named Examples. Shadow Obscura; Blazing Obscura; Raging Obscura; Ebonmist Obscura; Necrotic
Obscura; Inferno Obscura
