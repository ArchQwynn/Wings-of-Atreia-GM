Monitor
Monitor are reptilian predators.
Ecology & Behavior. Monitor are active hunters of tundra, ruined strongholds, frozen valleys, and windswept passes.
They follow game trails, water sources, and the edges of settlements where prey movement is predictable, shifting
between pursuit and ambush according to terrain. They are not mindless killers: a well-fed animal normally avoids a
costly fight, while starving, wounded, or territorial individuals can become far more dangerous.
Life in Atreia. War and migration make Monitor more dangerous. Abandoned farms, battlefield carrion, and displaced
prey can draw them close to roads and fortresses, turning an ordinary predator into a recurring frontier problem.
Locations. Beluslan
