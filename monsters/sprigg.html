Sprigg
Sprigg are sapient, organized small humanoids. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and organization vary by
group.
Ecology & Behavior. Sprigg communities are shaped less by a single universal culture than by the pressures of
wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as well as cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement
country. Bands, villages, camps, and war parties develop around dependable food, defensible ground, trade routes, and
the authority of leaders who can keep the group supplied. Tool use and local knowledge let them exploit resources that
nearby beasts cannot, and even a modest camp tends to contain caches, work spaces, paths, and signs of repeated
occupation. Forms such as Fighter, Sentry, and Wanderer are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or
specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. The wider wars of Atreia constantly intrude on Sprigg life. Armies requisition food, monsters displace
settlements, and ruined roads create opportunities for smuggling and salvage; communities respond by choosing
whichever combination of resistance, neutrality, or accommodation keeps them alive.
