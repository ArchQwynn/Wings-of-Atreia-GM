Sprigg

Source-locked complete migration.

Sprigg are sapient, organized small humanoids. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and organization vary by group.

Ecology & Behavior. Sprigg communities are shaped less by a single universal culture than by the pressures of wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as well as cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement country. Bands, villages, camps, and war parties develop around dependable food, defensible ground, trade routes, and the authority of leaders who can keep the group supplied. Tool use and local knowledge let them exploit resources that nearby beasts cannot, and even a modest camp tends to contain caches, work spaces, paths, and signs of repeated occupation. Forms such as Fighter, Sentry, and Wanderer are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. The wider wars of Atreia constantly intrude on Sprigg life. Armies requisition food, monsters displace settlements, and ruined roads create opportunities for smuggling and salvage; communities respond by choosing whichever combination of resistance, neutrality, or accommodation keeps them alive.

Locations. Poeta, Ishalgen

Variants and Named Examples. Fighter; Sentry; Wanderer; Gatherer; Worker; Outlaw; Spriggan Fighter; Spriggan Gatherer; Spriggan Looter; Spriggan Rustler; Spriggle Hunter; Spriggle Warden; Sku; Paku; Seku; Captain Subaku

Veteran+

Scatter. Once per turn, use this feature as a tactical movement, positioning, or teamwork benefit without adding another full-damage Rank Stage Feature action.

Elite+

Ambush. Once per turn, gain +2 to one attack roll when a relevant ally is within 5 ft of the target or when the creature began the turn unseen by that target. This bonus does not stack.

Boss+

Group Command. Cooldown 2. Choose up to two allied creatures of the same family within 30 ft that can hear the Boss. Each may move up to 10 ft or stand from Prone without spending its Action.
