Crynac
Crynacs are widespread reptilian wildlife across Eltnen and parts of Heiron, including deserts, mountain passes, springs,
and drake habitat. Some specialized forms conceal themselves and use venom.
Ecology & Behavior. In snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland as well as Balaurean wilds shaped
by war, immense ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether, Crynac occupy the role of mid-sized predators. They
feed on small and medium fauna, scavenge when convenient, and quickly learn which routes produce the easiest meals.
Their presence suppresses smaller scavengers and prey animals, making them an important indicator of a functioning
local food web. The Rattletail Crynac is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the
broader Crynac family.
Life in Atreia. Where Crynac ranges overlap with roads or livestock, Asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols as well as
Balaurean expeditions, fortress patrols, and frontier Daevas organize culls only after repeated attacks; most communities
prefer to drive the animals away rather than empty an area of a predator that keeps smaller pests in check.
Locations. Morheim, Greater Balaurea
Variants and Named Examples. Rattletail Crynac
