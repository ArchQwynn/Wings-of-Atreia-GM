Calydon
Calydons are aggressive boar-like sapiens. They are hunted for their meat and strong tusks.
Ecology & Behavior. Across arid wilderness, ancient ruins, broken canyons, and sparse scrub, Calydon live in
organized social groups whose size changes with circumstance. Small settlements can be practical and family-centered,
while threatened communities rapidly become more militarized, posting sentries and concentrating supplies behind
defensible positions. Competition between leaders can produce raiding and territorial violence, yet the same groups are
capable of truces, hostage exchanges, hired service, trade, and temporary alliances when survival makes cooperation
more valuable than pride.
Life in Atreia. The wider wars of Atreia constantly intrude on Calydon life. Armies requisition food, monsters displace
settlements, and ruined roads create opportunities for smuggling and salvage; communities respond by choosing
whichever combination of resistance, neutrality, or accommodation keeps them alive.
