Trico
Trico are fast-moving Atreian creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. The species thrives along the margins of snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen
woodland as well as dense Balaurean wilderness, Aether-rich growth, cliffs, and warfront routes, where vegetation, loose
stone, roots, and shallow burrows provide many places to hide. Individuals frequently freeze before fleeing, relying on
coloration and stillness until the last safe moment. The Young Furtive Trico is commonly treated as a recognizable local
or developmental expression of the broader Trico family.
Life in Atreia. Periods of war change the relationship between people and Trico. Empty farms and abandoned roads
create new habitat, while troop movement and Aetheric disturbances can displace populations into places where they
were once rare.
Locations. Morheim, Beluslan, Inggison
Variants and Named Examples. Young Furtive Trico
