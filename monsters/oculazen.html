Oculazen
Oculazen are ranged observer creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. The presence of Oculazen is usually felt before it is understood. In Balaurean wilds shaped by
war, immense ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether, witnesses report altered sounds, misleading movement,
unusual lights, or an oppressive sense of being observed before the creature finally reveals itself. Some can be driven
away when the condition sustaining them changes; others simply retreat and return later. This uncertainty is why old
stories about them tend to preserve practical taboos even when the explanations differ from village to village. The
Observing Oculazen is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Oculazen
family.
Life in Atreia. War can increase sightings of Oculazen by creating abandoned places, mass fear, disturbed Aether, and
unburied dead. For that reason, veteran Daevas may treat supernatural activity as an aftershock of conflict rather than
an isolated monster problem.
