Gargoyle

Source-locked complete migration.

Gargoyles can function as animated guardians rather than ordinary wildlife. Crouching Gargoyles are statues that guard the Fire Temple.

Ecology & Behavior. Unlike living fauna, Gargoyle do not need a stable food web. Their presence in woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and settled roads as well as harsh Balaurean valleys, fortress country, mineral ridges, and warfront routes usually points to a creator, a forgotten defense, a damaged Aetheric process, or a site whose magic continues to function long after its builders disappeared. Many remain dormant for years, conserving whatever force animates them until a boundary is crossed or a command condition is met. This makes apparently quiet ruins particularly dangerous. Forms such as Miraju Gargoyle, Impetusium Gargoyle, and Gurkel the Ancient are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Frontier settlements sometimes build around dormant Gargoyle only after generations of observation establish safe boundaries. A change in Aether flow, excavation, or warfare can awaken a guardian that local people had come to regard as harmless scenery.

Locations. Verteron, Heiron, Reshanta, Inggison, Gelkmaros

Variants and Named Examples. Miraju Gargoyle; Impetusium Gargoyle; Gurkel the Ancient

RANK FEATURES

Rank Features

Base

Claw. This establishes the family’s characteristic combat behavior and does not add another full-damage action.

Veteran+

Stone Guard. Reaction: when hit by an attack, gain +2 AC against that triggering attack, potentially causing it to miss.

Elite+

Diving Smash. Once per turn while airborne, move up to 10 ft immediately before or after the creature’s attack. This movement grants no additional attack.

Boss+

Terrifying Roar. Cooldown 2. Choose up to two allied creatures of the same family within 30 ft that can hear the Boss. Each may move up to 10 ft or stand from Prone without spending its Action.
