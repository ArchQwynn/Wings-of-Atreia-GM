Gargoyle
Gargoyles can function as animated guardians rather than ordinary wildlife. Crouching Gargoyles are statues that guard
the Fire Temple.
Ecology & Behavior. Unlike living fauna, Gargoyle do not need a stable food web. Their presence in woodlands, river
country, ruined approaches, and settled roads as well as harsh Balaurean valleys, fortress country, mineral ridges, and
warfront routes usually points to a creator, a forgotten defense, a damaged Aetheric process, or a site whose magic
continues to function long after its builders disappeared. Many remain dormant for years, conserving whatever force
animates them until a boundary is crossed or a command condition is met. This makes apparently quiet ruins particularly
dangerous. Forms such as Miraju Gargoyle, Impetusium Gargoyle, and Gurkel the Ancient are commonly interpreted as
regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Frontier settlements sometimes build around dormant Gargoyle only after generations of observation
establish safe boundaries. A change in Aether flow, excavation, or warfare can awaken a guardian that local people had
come to regard as harmless scenery.
Locations. Verteron, Heiron, Reshanta, Inggison, Gelkmaros
Variants and Named Examples. Miraju Gargoyle; Impetusium Gargoyle; Gurkel the Ancient
