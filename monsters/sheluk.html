Sheluk
Sheluks are hard-shelled arachnid-like creatures found in desert ruins and other hostile places. Stronger forms have
unusually hard shells and great strength, while webbing and flesh-eating behavior make them natural lair predators.
Ecology & Behavior. Sheluk are low-slung hunters and scavengers well suited to the sheltered ground of snowfields,
high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland. They favor cracks, roots, burrows, ruins, and dense vegetation where a
small body can remain unseen until prey is close. Predators learn to handle them cautiously, and experienced local
animals often give occupied nests or burrows a wide berth. The Angry Sheluk is commonly treated as a recognizable
local or developmental expression of the broader Sheluk family.
Life in Atreia. Local apothecaries pay for intact biological material from Sheluk, encouraging a small but risky trade in
venom sacs, shell, webbing, or glands. Poor handling can make the harvest more dangerous than the hunt itself.
