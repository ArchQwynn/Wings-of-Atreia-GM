Skyray
Skyrays are airborne creatures capable of controlling prey at range. Scaly Skyrays in Eracus Temple Cavern use snares
and venomous effects.
Ecology & Behavior. The species is most at home where open air meets broken terrain. In floating islands, exposed
ledges, fortress approaches, and unstable Aether currents as well as harsh Balaurean valleys, fortress country, mineral
ridges, and warfront routes, Skyray circle above clearings and ridges, using vertical space to escape larger ground
predators and to watch for disturbances below. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold around nests, repeatedly
passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Speartail Skyray, Scaly Skyray, and Spying
Skyray are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather
than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Feathers, membranes, eggshell, or shed hornlike material from Skyray sometimes enter local craft
traditions, but taking them from an active nesting site is far more dangerous than collecting naturally discarded material.
Locations. Reshanta, Inggison, Gelkmaros
Variants and Named Examples. Speartail Skyray; Scaly Skyray; Spying Skyray; Shielded Skyray; Venomous Skyray;
Thorntail Skyray; Skyray Hatchling; Dekita
