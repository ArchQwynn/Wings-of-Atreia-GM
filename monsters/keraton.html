Keraton
Keratons are huge horn-covered creatures of the Eracus deserts. Hookhorn Keratons ram opponents with the horns
covering their bodies.
Ecology & Behavior. Across dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins, Keraton function as major
landscape animals. They open trails through dense growth, churn soil around watering places, and leave feeding
grounds that are quickly used by smaller creatures. Their feeding pressure can reshape a local patch of wilderness, and
abandoned resting places often become seed-rich clearings used by smaller fauna. Forms such as Blackmane Keraton,
Hookhorn Keraton, and Ruby Keraton are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of
the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Large Keraton are economically significant wherever they occur. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols prize
hide, horn, shell, bone, meat, or sinew, but mature animals are rarely worth provoking without preparation.
Locations. Eltnen
Variants and Named Examples. Blackmane Keraton; Hookhorn Keraton; Ruby Keraton; Needlethorn Keraton
