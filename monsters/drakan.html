Drakan
Drakan are the foundational Balaur people. Their history is inseparable from the Balaur empire: originally created as
protectors, they became an organized conquering power, with modern Drakan commonly serving as guards, soldiers,
specialists, and officers.
Ecology & Behavior. Drakan society is military, hierarchical, and technically sophisticated, but individual Drakan are not
interchangeable soldiers. Officers, engineers, scouts, priests, labor specialists, beast handlers, and administrators all
exist behind the front line, and each legion develops its own habits under the influence of commanders and the Dragon
Lord it ultimately serves. Within Balaurea, Drakan fortifications are supported by supply roads, workshops, watch
stations, cultivated resources, and subject peoples. In the Abyss and on expeditions into Elysea or Asmodae, their
discipline lets comparatively small forces hold dangerous ground while waiting for reinforcements or pursuing specific
strategic goals.
Life in Atreia. Elyos and Asmodian Daevas most often meet Drakan as enemy combatants, but intelligence work
reveals a more complicated world of rival legions, competing officers, deserters, prisoners, informants, and individuals
whose loyalty is pragmatic rather than absolute. Any temporary cooperation remains dangerous, yet the long war creates
situations in which information, survival, or a common threat can matter more than ideology for a few crucial hours.
Locations. Heiron, Beluslan, Reshanta, Inggison, Gelkmaros, Silentera Canyon, Greater Balaurea
