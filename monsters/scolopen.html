Scolopen
Scolopens are poisonous crawling predators. Morheim forms can hide themselves, strike from behind, and deliver
stunning poison.
Ecology & Behavior. Scolopen are low-slung hunters and scavengers well suited to the sheltered ground of dry
grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as arid wilderness, ancient ruins, broken canyons, and
sparse scrub. They favor cracks, roots, burrows, ruins, and dense vegetation where a small body can remain unseen
until prey is close. Their life cycle tends to produce sudden local population booms when food and warmth are plentiful,
followed by sharp declines when prey becomes scarce. Forms such as Poison Tail Scolopen, Rattletail Scolopen, and
Blacktail Scolopen are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader
family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols treat dense concentrations of Scolopen as both a hazard and a
resource. Alchemists and field medics value carefully collected venom, glands, silk, chitin, or spores, while ordinary
travelers mostly care about keeping them away from bedrolls and provisions.
Locations. Eltnen, Theobomos
Variants and Named Examples. Poison Tail Scolopen; Rattletail Scolopen; Blacktail Scolopen
