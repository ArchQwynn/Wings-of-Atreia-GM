Petrahulk
Petrahulk are massive stone brutes.
Ecology & Behavior. A Petrahulk is less a species than a recurring Atreian design: matter given directive and motion.
Examples found in dense Balaurean wilderness, Aether-rich growth, cliffs, and warfront routes as well as Balaurean
wilds shaped by war, immense ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether may differ greatly in age and appearance
while still sharing the same basic tendency to protect, patrol, or violently respond to intrusion. Many remain dormant for
years, conserving whatever force animates them until a boundary is crossed or a command condition is met. This makes
apparently quiet ruins particularly dangerous. Forms such as Wrathful Petrahulk, Mighty Petrahulk, and Destroyer
Hamitemon are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family
rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Frontier settlements sometimes build around dormant Petrahulk only after generations of observation
establish safe boundaries. A change in Aether flow, excavation, or warfare can awaken a guardian that local people had
come to regard as harmless scenery.
Locations. Inggison, Gelkmaros, Greater Balaurea
Variants and Named Examples. Wrathful Petrahulk; Mighty Petrahulk; Destroyer Hamitemon
