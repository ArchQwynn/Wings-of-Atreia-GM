Brax
Brax are territorial beasts.
Ecology & Behavior. Brax occupy a flexible niche throughout wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as
well as cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote settlement country, feeding and sheltering wherever their body
plan gives them an advantage. Local populations adapt quickly to differences in climate, prey, and vegetation. Their
behavior changes noticeably with food pressure and disturbance: animals near settled roads are more wary, while
remote populations can be curious or aggressively territorial toward unfamiliar movement. Forms such as Graybristle
Brax, Lake Brax, and Graybristle Braxie (juvenile) are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized
expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Periods of war change the relationship between people and Brax. Empty farms and abandoned roads
create new habitat, while troop movement and Aetheric disturbances can displace populations into places where they
were once rare.
Locations. Poeta, Ishalgen
Variants and Named Examples. Graybristle Brax; Lake Brax; Graybristle Braxie (juvenile)
