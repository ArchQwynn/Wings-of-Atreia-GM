Temptress

Source-locked complete migration.

Temptresses are supernatural predators associated with deception and weakening prey. The Alluring Temptress is a legendary monster that lures Daevas into Eracus Temple Cavern and saps their strength.

Ecology & Behavior. Temptress are creatures in which Aether, emotion, memory, or predatory instinct has become as important as flesh. They are drawn to places in dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland where ordinary boundaries feel thin: ruins, lonely roads, sites of death, powerful dreams, or regions saturated with unstable Aether. Aether-sensitive Daevas sometimes notice their presence earlier than ordinary travelers, but awareness does not necessarily make the encounter safer; these beings are often adapted to exploiting attention, fear, or curiosity. Forms such as Selcuris Dreamweaver, and Lichna the Cruel are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Encounters with Temptress leave a cultural mark disproportionate to their numbers. A single dangerous site can alter a road, create a local taboo, or inspire a shrine intended less to worship the creature than to keep travelers away from it.

Locations. Eltnen, Morheim

Variants and Named Examples. Selcuris Dreamweaver; Lichna the Cruel
