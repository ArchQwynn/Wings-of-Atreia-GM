Slime
Slime are amorphous absorbers.
Ecology & Behavior. Slime are amorphous organisms that prosper where organic refuse, moisture, and Aetheric
residue collect in woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and settled roads. They feed by surrounding material and
breaking it down, leaving unusually clean stone alongside patches where nothing easily survives. They have little reason
to chase distant prey, but repeated feeding can make a colony large enough to block passages or swallow caches,
corpses, and abandoned equipment. Their simple behavior makes them predictable until they encounter an unfamiliar
magical substance. Forms such as Contaminated Slime, Rainbow Slime, and Poisonous Bubblegut are commonly
interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated
creatures.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols sometimes tolerate small Slime populations as natural refuse-eaters
in remote ruins, but they are destroyed quickly when they spread toward wells, storehouses, or occupied tunnels.
Alchemists value samples precisely because the same digestive material that ruins equipment can dissolve substances
difficult to process by ordinary means.
Locations. Verteron
Variants and Named Examples. Contaminated Slime; Rainbow Slime; Poisonous Bubblegut
