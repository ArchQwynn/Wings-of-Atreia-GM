Spiner
Spiner are spined creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Spiner occupy a flexible niche throughout dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins,
feeding and sheltering wherever their body plan gives them an advantage. Local populations adapt quickly to differences
in climate, prey, and vegetation. Their behavior changes noticeably with food pressure and disturbance: animals near
settled roads are more wary, while remote populations can be curious or aggressively territorial toward unfamiliar
movement. Forms such as Snapping Spiner, and Black Mask Spiner are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related,
or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols know Spiner as part of the ordinary living landscape rather than as a
monster that must always be fought. Hunters, herders, and scouts learn its tracks and calls because they reveal water,
predators, weather shifts, and the recent passage of larger creatures.
Locations. Eltnen
Variants and Named Examples. Snapping Spiner; Black Mask Spiner
