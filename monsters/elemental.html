Elemental
Elemental are supernatural elemental beings capable of ranged attacks and battlefield control.
Ecology & Behavior. Elementals are living expressions of Atreia's Aether-saturated environment. Their bodies take on
the substance and motion of the place that gives rise to them: earth spirits gather stone and soil into weighty forms, wind
spirits exist as motion around a shifting core, and water, frost, flame, storm, or mud spirits manifest according to the
dominant local conditions. They do not form one biological species, but Daevas group them together because the same
principle—Aether giving temporary will to an element—appears across the shattered world.
Life in Atreia. Atreians rarely regard every elemental as an enemy. Small manifestations can be treated as omens of
healthy springs, changing weather, or unstable Aether, while larger ones may make a road, mine, ruin, or battlefield
unusable. Spiritmasters and scholars seek to understand the conditions that attract them; farmers and soldiers are more
practical, learning which manifestations can be ignored and which must be dispersed before they damage crops,
fortifications, or travelers.
