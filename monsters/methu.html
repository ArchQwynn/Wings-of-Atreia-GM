Methu
Methu are territorial beasts.
Ecology & Behavior. Methu occupy a flexible niche throughout cold forest margins, rocky valleys, and remote
settlement country as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads, feeding and sheltering
wherever their body plan gives them an advantage. Local populations adapt quickly to differences in climate, prey, and
vegetation. Their behavior changes noticeably with food pressure and disturbance: animals near settled roads are more
wary, while remote populations can be curious or aggressively territorial toward unfamiliar movement.
Life in Atreia. Periods of war change the relationship between people and Methu. Empty farms and abandoned roads
create new habitat, while troop movement and Aetheric disturbances can displace populations into places where they
were once rare.
Locations. Ishalgen, Altgard
