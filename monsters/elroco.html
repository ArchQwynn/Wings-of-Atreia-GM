Elroco
Elrocos are common wildlife seen across both Elysea and Asmodae, including forests, villages, and low-level wilderness.
They are broadly distributed across both Elysea and Asmodae.
Ecology & Behavior. The species thrives along the margins of wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths as
well as remote woodland, damp lowlands, coastward country, and abandoned tracts, where vegetation, loose stone,
roots, and shallow burrows provide many places to hide. Individuals frequently freeze before fleeing, relying on coloration
and stillness until the last safe moment.
Life in Atreia. Elyos hunters, farmers, and patrols as well as asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols know Elroco as part
of the ordinary living landscape rather than as a monster that must always be fought. Hunters, herders, and scouts learn
its tracks and calls because they reveal water, predators, weather shifts, and the recent passage of larger creatures.
Locations. Poeta, Verteron, Ishalgen, Altgard, Brusthonin
