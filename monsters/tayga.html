Tayga
Taygas are predatory animals of Asmodae and can also be raised or kept by people. Wild forms occur around Black
Claw Village and the Morheim snowfields, while young Tayga can also be raised or kept by people.
Ecology & Behavior. In cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads as well as tundra, ruined
strongholds, frozen valleys, and windswept passes, Tayga occupy the role of mid-sized predators. They feed on small
and medium fauna, scavenge when convenient, and quickly learn which routes produce the easiest meals. Where
several individuals overlap, scent marks, calls, and ritual threat displays often settle disputes before claws or teeth are
used. Forms such as Tamed Tayga, Wild Tayga, and Wailing Tayga are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related,
or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Where Tayga ranges overlap with roads or livestock, Asmodian hunters, settlers, and patrols organize
culls only after repeated attacks; most communities prefer to drive the animals away rather than empty an area of a
predator that keeps smaller pests in check.
Locations. Altgard, Morheim, Beluslan
Variants and Named Examples. Tamed Tayga; Wild Tayga; Wailing Tayga
