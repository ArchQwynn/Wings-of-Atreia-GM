Crestlich
Crestlich are large birdlike animals with strong regional adaptation. Desert Crestlich nest in arid wastes and are notably
noisy; some ground-dwelling lineages have vestigial wings and cannot fly, while larger forms are valued for meat and
feathers.
Ecology & Behavior. Across dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins, Crestlich spend much of their
lives moving between high perches and open feeding grounds. Their calls carry farther than their bodies are usually
visible, making a colony or nesting pair easier to hear than to approach. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold
around nests, repeatedly passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Desert Crestlich,
Bladebeak Crestlich, and Redthroat Crestlich are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized
expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Because Daevas themselves travel through the air, Crestlich are more than background wildlife. Elyos
hunters, farmers, and patrols learn the locations of major roosts and avoid crossing them at low altitude, especially
during nesting season.
Locations. Eltnen
Variants and Named Examples. Desert Crestlich; Bladebeak Crestlich; Redthroat Crestlich; Imposing Crestlich
