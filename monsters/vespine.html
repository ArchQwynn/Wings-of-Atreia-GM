Vespine

Source-locked complete migration.

Vespine are flying venomous insectoids.

Ecology & Behavior. Vespine are low-slung hunters and scavengers well suited to the sheltered ground of dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins as well as harsh Balaurean valleys, fortress country, mineral ridges, and warfront routes. They favor cracks, roots, burrows, ruins, and dense vegetation where a small body can remain unseen until prey is close. They are important consumers of insects, vermin, and carrion, but their proximity to camps and storehouses makes them a recurring nuisance to travelers. Forms such as Poison Wing Vespine, and Queen Vespine are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Settlements near Vespine habitat clear brush, burn abandoned nests, and keep paths open around wells and storehouses. Skilled hunters, however, sometimes preserve remote colonies because their presence suppresses other vermin.

Locations. Eltnen, Heiron, Theobomos, Brusthonin, Gelkmaros

Variants and Named Examples. Poison Wing Vespine; Queen Vespine
