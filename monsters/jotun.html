Jotun
Jotun are ancient mechanical giants crafted by Aion as a response to the Balaur threat. Surviving Jotun and their
creations continue to guard their workshops with extraordinary persistence, in some cases behaving as though the
Cataclysm never interrupted their original purpose.
Ecology & Behavior. Jotun are surviving representatives of an ancient people whose mastery of machinery and Aether
predates much of the present order of Atreia. Their workshops were built on a scale suited to giants and filled with
mechanisms that could continue operating without daily supervision. A surviving Jotun may therefore treat a ruined
complex as an active workplace, maintaining routines, guarding unfinished projects, and judging intruders according to
rules established centuries before the current war.
Life in Atreia. Modern Daevas approach Jotun sites with equal parts fear and scholarly hunger. A functioning workshop
can contain knowledge capable of changing warfare, engineering, or the understanding of pre-Cataclysm Atreia, yet
stripping such a place for parts may awaken guardians or destroy information that cannot be replaced. Jotun themselves
need not view Elyos, Asmodians, or Balaur through modern political categories; to them, all three may simply be
latecomers trespassing in a task that was never formally ended.
