Strigik
Strigik are mobile aerial humanoids capable of ranged combat. They are intelligent beings whose equipment and
organization vary by group.
Ecology & Behavior. Strigik society is built around cooperation, reputation, and access to resources. In dense
Balaurean wilderness, Aether-rich growth, cliffs, and warfront routes as well as Balaurean wilds shaped by war, immense
ruins, strange vegetation, and powerful Aether, the ability to secure food, tools, shelter, and safe passage matters as
much as personal strength, and successful leaders are usually those who can provide all four. They adapt readily to
stronger neighbors, sometimes serving, trading, hiding, or relocating rather than meeting every threat with direct
violence. The Chieftain Owloro is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader
Strigik family.
Life in Atreia. Daevas encounter Strigik not only as enemies but as guides, laborers, traders, rivals, refugees, prisoners,
mercenaries, and local powers. The reaction of a particular group depends on its recent history with Elyos, Asmodians,
Balaur, and neighboring peoples.
Locations. Inggison, Gelkmaros, Greater Balaurea
Variants and Named Examples. Chieftain Owloro
