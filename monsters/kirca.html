Kirca
Kircas are tiny squirrel-like animals. They are small and harmless.
Ecology & Behavior. The species thrives along the margins of cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended
roads, where vegetation, loose stone, roots, and shallow burrows provide many places to hide. Their alarm behavior can
warn other nearby animals, and experienced hunters sometimes read sudden silence or flight as a sign that something
larger is approaching.
Life in Atreia. The species appears in local stories precisely because it is familiar. Children learn to recognize it, hunters
know which parts are useful, and patrols understand when its unusual behavior signals a greater disturbance in the
region.
