Griffon
Griffons combine a lion's body with an eagle-like head and wings. Wild griffons are territorial and protective, with keen
sight and hearing; they are primarily seen by day, defend themselves fiercely, and can be tamed and trained as guards.
Ecology & Behavior. Across deep forests, overgrown ruins, mountain country, and abandoned roads as well as floating
islands, exposed ledges, fortress approaches, and unstable Aether currents, Griffon spend much of their lives moving
between high perches and open feeding grounds. Their calls carry farther than their bodies are usually visible, making a
colony or nesting pair easier to hear than to approach. Territorial adults can become surprisingly bold around nests,
repeatedly passing close to intruders until they retreat or are driven off. Forms such as Griffon, Tamed Griffon, and Griffo
(juvenile) are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family
rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Feathers, membranes, eggshell, or shed hornlike material from Griffon sometimes enter local craft
traditions, but taking them from an active nesting site is far more dangerous than collecting naturally discarded material.
Locations. Heiron, Reshanta
Variants and Named Examples. Griffon; Tamed Griffon; Griffo (juvenile); Dancing Feather; Griffonix; Beleffon
