Boggart
Boggart are elusive skirmishing creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Boggart occupy a flexible niche throughout deep forests, overgrown ruins, mountain country, and abandoned roads, feeding and sheltering wherever their body plan gives them an advantage. Local populations adapt quickly to differences in climate, prey, and vegetation. Their behavior changes noticeably with food pressure and disturbance: animals near settled roads are more wary, while remote populations can be curious or aggressively territorial toward unfamiliar movement. The Snickering Boggart is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Boggart family.
Life in Atreia. Periods of war change the relationship between people and Boggart. Empty farms and abandoned roads create new habitat, while troop movement and Aetheric disturbances can displace populations into places where they were once rare.
