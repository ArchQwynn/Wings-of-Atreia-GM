Pretor
Pretor are artificial creatures created by the Lepharists.
Ecology & Behavior. A Pretor is less a species than a recurring Atreian design: matter given directive and motion.
Examples found in snowfields, high valleys, rocky slopes, and frozen woodland may differ greatly in age and appearance
while still sharing the same basic tendency to protect, patrol, or violently respond to intrusion. Many remain dormant for
years, conserving whatever force animates them until a boundary is crossed or a command condition is met. This makes
apparently quiet ruins particularly dangerous. Forms such as Pretor Sentinel, and Experimental Pretors
(instance/deferred context noted) are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the
same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Daevas often meet Pretor during salvage operations, ruin exploration, or attempts to secure ancient
infrastructure. Destroying one may solve the immediate danger while also erasing clues to the people or system that
created it.
