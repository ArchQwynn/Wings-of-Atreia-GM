Abex
Abex are horned animals of Atreia's wooded regions. Grove Abex are recorded in Daminu Forest, where their hides are
useful for clothing.
Ecology & Behavior. Abex are distinctive four-eyed, four-horned ungulates whose paired eyes give them an unusually
broad awareness of both the horizon and the ground immediately around their feet. Their long legs, narrow bodies,
heavy curling horns, shaggy neck manes, and whorled coat markings make them well suited to broken woodland and
upland terrain. Herds browse grasses, leaves, shoots, and low branches, moving between familiar water and feeding
grounds while older animals watch the edges of the group for predators.
Life in Atreia. In Atreia, Abex are familiar enough to be livestock competitors, game animals, and signs of healthy
wilderness rather than automatic monsters. Elyos and Asmodian hunters use hide, horn, sinew, and meat, while
travelers sometimes follow old Abex trails through difficult forest country. Herds displaced by war or Aetheric disturbance
can wander into cropland or roads, and a cornered adult is fully capable of driving off predators—or careless Daevas—
with a violent charge.
Locations. Poeta, Verteron, Morheim, Brusthonin
Variants and Named Examples. Mountain Abex; Stubborn Abex; Obstinate Abex; Fauron
