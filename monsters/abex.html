Abex

Medium Beast · Durability: Normal · Combat Identity: Horned charger

Ecology & Behavior

Abex are familiar enough to be livestock competitors, game animals, and signs of healthy wilderness rather than automatic monsters. Their long legs, narrow bodies, heavy curling horns, shaggy neck manes, and whorled coat markings make them well suited to broken woodland and upland terrain. Herds browse grasses, leaves, shoots, and low branches, moving between familiar water and feeding grounds while older animals watch the edges of the group for predators.

Life in Atreia

Elyos and Asmodian hunters use hide, horn, sinew, and meat. Travelers sometimes follow old Abex trails through difficult forest country. Herds displaced by war or Aetheric disturbance can wander into cropland or roads, and a cornered adult is fully capable of driving off predators—or careless Daevas—with a violent charge.

Rank Features

Base — Gore. This is the family’s characteristic attack pattern and remains inside the normal action damage budget.

Veteran+ — Charge. Once per turn, after moving at least 15 ft toward a hostile creature and hitting it, force a STR save. On a failure, push the target 5 ft horizontally.

Elite+ — Trampling Gore. Cooldown 2. Make one signature attack. On a hit, deal about 125–150% of the normal Band/Rank damage.

Boss+ — Dominating Charge. When the Boss successfully uses Charge, increase the forced movement from 5 ft to 10 ft.

Actions

Gore (Piercing; reach 5 ft). Use the selected Band/Rank ATK against AC; on a hit, deal the selected normal DMG.

Boss Action

Stampeding Advance. Move up to half its Speed toward a visible hostile creature. It makes no attack as part of this Boss Action. This is ordinary movement and can provoke Opportunity Attacks.
