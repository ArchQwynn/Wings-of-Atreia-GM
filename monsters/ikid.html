Ikid

Source-locked complete migration.

Ikid are aggressive beasts.

Ecology & Behavior. In cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads, Ikid occupy the role of mid-sized predators. They feed on small and medium fauna, scavenge when convenient, and quickly learn which routes produce the easiest meals. Their presence suppresses smaller scavengers and prey animals, making them an important indicator of a functioning local food web. The Karat Ikid is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Ikid family.

Life in Atreia. War and migration make Ikid more dangerous. Abandoned farms, battlefield carrion, and displaced prey can draw them close to roads and fortresses, turning an ordinary predator into a recurring frontier problem.

Locations. Altgard
