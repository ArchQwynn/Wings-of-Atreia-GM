Malodor
Malodors are carnivorous plants. Swamp Malodors grow beneath large trees in Mahindel Swamp and attack at range
with thorns.
Ecology & Behavior. Malodor occupy a flexible niche throughout woodlands, river country, ruined approaches, and
settled roads, feeding and sheltering wherever their body plan gives them an advantage. Local populations adapt quickly
to differences in climate, prey, and vegetation. Their behavior changes noticeably with food pressure and disturbance:
animals near settled roads are more wary, while remote populations can be curious or aggressively territorial toward
unfamiliar movement. Forms such as Crimson Malodor, Grove Malodor, and Swamp Malodor are commonly interpreted
as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. The species appears in local stories precisely because it is familiar. Children learn to recognize it, hunters
know which parts are useful, and patrols understand when its unusual behavior signals a greater disturbance in the
region.
Locations. Verteron
