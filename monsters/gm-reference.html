Using the Monster Compendium

Quick guidance for selecting and running creatures without duplicating player-facing rules.

Choose by Family

Use the family index to find the creature type appropriate to the environment or story.

Choose by Rank

Use Standard, Veteran, Elite, or Boss to select the intended encounter tier.

Use Habitat

Match creature entries to established regions and environments.

Read Behavior

GM-facing behavior and tactics should help the creature feel distinct at the table.

Prepare Loot

Use the entry's finalized loot/reference notes where provided, or the separate GM tables.

Respect Canon

Do not create alternate mechanics here when an authoritative statblock already exists.
