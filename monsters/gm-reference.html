Running Monsters

Monster Compendium v2.6 reference rules. These notes summarize how the bestiary is intended to be used at the table.

PC Bands

Band I: Levels 1–5. Band II: 6–10. Band III: 11–15. Band IV: 16–20. Ordinary creatures are calibrated for a standard baseline of five PCs.

Ranks

Standard, Veteran, Elite, and Boss are the four ordinary creature ranks.

No CR / XP Budget

Wings of Atreia does not use Challenge Rating or XP encounter budgets for ordinary creatures.

Explicit-Only Traits

Creature type, anatomy, rank, lineage, and appearance do not grant hidden mechanics. Special traits exist only when explicitly printed.

Boss Actions

Every Boss-rank ordinary creature has one printed Boss Action. It is used at the end of another creature’s turn and refreshes at the start of the Boss’s turn.

Ability Modifiers

Ability modifiers govern Initiative, unlisted checks and saves, passive scores, and explicit interactions. They do not recalculate AC, HP, ATK, Save DC, or normal damage.

STATBLOCK PRINCIPLE

The printed statblock is the mechanical authority. Variants inherit the nearest approved parent chassis unless their entry explicitly states otherwise.
