Stoffu
Stoffu are durable beasts.
Ecology & Behavior. Stoffu are heavy-bodied animals that claim broad feeding ranges through cold uplands, ice-
rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. Their size lets them push through brush, break crusted ground, and reach
food that smaller grazers or browsers cannot easily exploit. Young animals remain close to larger adults, while mature
specimens are much more willing to stand their ground against predators. The Stoneskin Stoffu is commonly treated as a
recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Stoffu family.
Life in Atreia. Communities near Stoffu habitat build fences and storage areas with the animal's strength in mind. A
single frightened adult can damage crops or structures without behaving as a deliberate attacker.
Locations. Altgard
