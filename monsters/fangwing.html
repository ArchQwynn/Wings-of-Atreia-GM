Fangwing
Fangwings are aerial wildlife associated with caverns and other vertical spaces. Blade Fangwings inhabit the Eracus
Temple Cavern, where their pointed attacks make them hazardous flyers and their wings are valued.
Ecology & Behavior. Fangwing favor elevated nesting or resting sites overlooking dry grasslands, badlands, springs,
and sun-baked ruins. They use height as protection and as a way to survey feeding grounds, descending only when
prey, carrion, water, or nesting material draws them down. When alarmed they rarely commit immediately to a close
fight; they wheel, reposition, and strike at exposed targets before climbing away again. Forms such as Swift Fangwing,
Ruthless Fangwing, and Blackclaw Fangwing are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized
expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Feathers, membranes, eggshell, or shed hornlike material from Fangwing sometimes enter local craft
traditions, but taking them from an active nesting site is far more dangerous than collecting naturally discarded material.
Locations. Eltnen
Variants and Named Examples. Swift Fangwing; Ruthless Fangwing; Blackclaw Fangwing; Long-eared Fangwing
