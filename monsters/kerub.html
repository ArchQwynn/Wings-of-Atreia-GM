Kerub

Source-locked complete migration.

Kerubs are hunter-gatherers with loose tribal organization found throughout Elysea. Different Kerub groups occupy distinct territories in Poeta and Eltnen.

Ecology & Behavior. Kerub communities are shaped less by a single universal culture than by the pressures of wooded valleys, farm clearings, and old forest paths. Bands, villages, camps, and war parties develop around dependable food, defensible ground, trade routes, and the authority of leaders who can keep the group supplied. Movement between settlements carries stories, goods, techniques, and grudges, giving even isolated groups a wider awareness of regional events than outsiders often expect. Forms such as Smallhorn Kerub, Wandering Kerub, and Bigfoot Kerubar are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated creatures.

Life in Atreia. Daevas encounter Kerub not only as enemies but as guides, laborers, traders, rivals, refugees, prisoners, mercenaries, and local powers. The reaction of a particular group depends on its recent history with Elyos, Asmodians, Balaur, and neighboring peoples.

Locations. Poeta

Variants and Named Examples. Smallhorn Kerub; Wandering Kerub; Bigfoot Kerubar; Digging Kerub; Paruru Slowlegs
