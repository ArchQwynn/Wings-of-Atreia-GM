Tog
Tog are aggressive beasts.
Ecology & Behavior. Tog are active hunters of cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. They
follow game trails, water sources, and the edges of settlements where prey movement is predictable, shifting between
pursuit and ambush according to terrain. Where several individuals overlap, scent marks, calls, and ritual threat displays
often settle disputes before claws or teeth are used. Forms such as Stumptail Tog, Wild Spotted Tog, and Wild Striped
Tog are commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather
than entirely unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. War and migration make Tog more dangerous. Abandoned farms, battlefield carrion, and displaced prey
can draw them close to roads and fortresses, turning an ordinary predator into a recurring frontier problem.
Locations. Altgard
Variants and Named Examples. Stumptail Tog; Wild Spotted Tog; Wild Striped Tog; Fierce Tog; Shade Tog; Wild Tog
