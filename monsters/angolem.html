Angolem

Large Construct · Durability: Heavy · Combat Identity: Magical stone creature

Ecology & Behavior

Angolem are magical stone creatures.

Angolem are most often encountered where worked stone, old machinery, mineral deposits, or concentrated Aether have been shaped into something capable of movement. In cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads, inactive specimens can be mistaken for architecture or debris until their original purpose is disturbed. Many remain dormant for years, conserving whatever force animates them until a boundary is crossed or a command condition is met. This makes apparently quiet ruins particularly dangerous. The Blackened Angolem is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Angolem family.

Life in Atreia

Daevas often meet Angolem during salvage operations, ruin exploration, or attempts to secure ancient infrastructure. Destroying one may solve the immediate danger while also erasing clues to the people or system that created it.
