Ribbit
Ribbit are amphibious creatures.
Ecology & Behavior. Ribbit occupy wet ground, shorelines, pools, and water-cut hollows within wooded valleys, farm
clearings, and old forest paths as well as cold uplands, ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads. Much of their
activity follows moisture: they forage most confidently where mud, reeds, submerged stone, or shallow water gives them
cover. Their feeding helps clear carrion and soft organic debris from waterways, though dense populations can also strip
spawning beds and shallow pools of smaller life. Forms such as Mud Ribbit, Cute Ribbit, and Strange Ribbit are
commonly interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely
unrelated creatures.
Life in Atreia. Aetheric storms and floods can relocate Ribbit unexpectedly, so a pool considered safe one season may
hold a breeding group the next. Frontier patrols therefore treat unfamiliar water with more caution than its size suggests.
Locations. Poeta, Ishalgen, Altgard
Variants and Named Examples. Mud Ribbit; Cute Ribbit; Strange Ribbit; Sanga Ribbit
