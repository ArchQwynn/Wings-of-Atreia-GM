Frightcorn
Frightcorn are mobile plantlike ambushers.
Ecology & Behavior. The Frightcorn life cycle is tied to disturbed soil and concentrated organic matter in cold uplands,
ice-rimmed lakes, forests, and defended roads as well as remote woodland, damp lowlands, coastward country, and
abandoned tracts. Spores, seeds, or root fragments can remain dormant for long periods before a favorable season
produces a sudden cluster of active growths. Small animals often live around mature colonies, feeding on shed material
while avoiding the dangerous central growths. This creates pockets of unexpectedly rich ecology around an otherwise
threatening organism. Forms such as Unripe Frightcorn, Rotten Frightcorn, and Ripened Frightcorn are commonly
interpreted as regional, age-related, or specialized expressions of the same broader family rather than entirely unrelated
creatures.
Life in Atreia. Aetheric disturbances can cause Frightcorn patches to appear in places previously considered safe.
Patrols often read a sudden bloom as evidence that the local environment has changed, even when the plants
themselves are not the greater threat.
Locations. Altgard, Brusthonin
Variants and Named Examples. Unripe Frightcorn; Rotten Frightcorn; Ripened Frightcorn
