Baku
Baku are large Atreian beasts.
Ecology & Behavior. Baku are heavy-bodied animals that claim broad feeding ranges through dry grasslands, badlands, springs, and sun-baked ruins. Their size lets them push through brush, break crusted ground, and reach food that smaller grazers or browsers cannot easily exploit. Most would rather warn an intruder than pursue it, but once committed they use weight, horns, shell, or sheer momentum to clear space around themselves. The Bighorn Baku is commonly treated as a recognizable local or developmental expression of the broader Baku family.
Life in Atreia. Road builders and farmers pay attention to old Baku trails because the animals repeatedly choose the easiest passages through difficult country. Those same routes can become dangerous when a herd is moving or defending young.
