Dukaki
Dukaki are best understood as a Kobold community rather than an unrelated species. Dukaki Kobolds live under Tursin
Krall oversight, with bruisers, runners, scrappers, trackers, watchers, and workers among their communities.
Ecology & Behavior. Dukaki communities are Kobold settlements shaped by generations of pressure from stronger
neighbors, especially the Tursin Krall. Their settlements divide labor visibly: diggers open ground and maintain paths,
woodcutters and workers gather necessities, keeneyes and sentinels watch approaches, and brawlers or smackers
protect the community when intimidation fails. This organization is not evidence of a naturally warlike people so much as
the practical response of a small population living under coercion and frequent danger.
Life in Atreia. Elyos who encounter Dukaki near Poeta and Verteron may find frightened laborers, aggressive patrols,
opportunistic thieves, or individuals willing to trade information about Krall movements. Some Dukaki cling to Tursin
authority because it is the only protection they know; others resent it and quietly assist outsiders who seem capable of
weakening their overseers. Food, tools, and promises of safe passage can matter more to them than speeches about
factional loyalty.
Locations. Poeta, Verteron
