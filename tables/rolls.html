DESIGN RULE

Randomize presentation without randomizing canon

Roll tables should produce only what their source explicitly permits. Common equipment naming is a flavor utility; Named Relics remain story-driven; and ordinary loot comes from what creatures, NPCs, caches, armories, or objectives actually contain.
