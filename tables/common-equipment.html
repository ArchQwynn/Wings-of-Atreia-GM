Atreian Common Equipment d100 Tables

27 rollable d100 naming tables containing 2,700 Common equipment names. These are naming utilities only.

How to Use

  1. Choose the equipment category.
  2. Roll 1d100 or choose a result. Read 00 as 100.
  3. Apply the name only. A rolled name does not grant a bonus, hidden property, rarity increase, elemental effect, or special ability.
  4. Reroll or choose another result when the suggested provenance does not fit the item.

Common equipment names may reference regions, institutions, workshops, expeditions, Abyss locations, and crafting vocabulary. The name itself does not establish additional setting canon.
