GM REFERENCE · REWARDS

Loot & Rewards

Use actual possessions and story context rather than a universal treasure-by-level formula.

Core Loot Principle

Enemies drop only what they actually possess. Equipment keeps its real quality. Unused consumables can be looted when actually present. Aether gear can be recovered from an NPC who actually owned it.

There is no universal random loot table, mandatory treasure-by-level table, or automatic high-level loot inflation.

ORDINARY REWARDS

Routine & Mission Rewards

Kinah, consumables, information, contacts, access, favors, Refined Aether, sanctuary, story equipment, captured objectives, or other scenario-specific rewards.

EQUIPMENT

Actual-Owner Loot

Recover weapons, armor, implements, wings, or other equipment when the defeated or displaced owner actually possessed the item.

RELICS

Named Relics

Named Relics are story-bearing discoveries. Do not generate them from a universal random-loot table; introduce them through recovery, inheritance, major quests, relic sites, faction operations, or other meaningful events.

WARFRONT

Strategic Rewards

Warfront rewards may include scenario-specific War Assets, captured objectives, supplies, or other campaign-level consequences.

GM GUIDANCE

Reward the situation, not a level chart

Consider what was present, who owned it, why it matters now, the local economy, faction access, rarity, condition, and the consequences of possession. Legendary items have no ordinary market price.
