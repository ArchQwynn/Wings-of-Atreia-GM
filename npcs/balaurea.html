World and Regional Anchors

The compendium identifies the following as major anchors associated with Balaurean regions. Entries here summarize their listed campaign functions without adding new secrets or outcomes.

MastariusGelkmarosMajor Asmodian defender of Gelkmaros’s forward line. A regional military anchor for defense and Warfront support; later survival crises may place him in rally, casualty, or evacuation-protection roles according to Locked History.
RichelleGelkmarosGelkmaros expeditionary commander for logistics, strategic defense, and Balaurea operations. Later arcs use her for coalition, Warfront, and survival planning.
ValettaGelkmarosSenior Fatebound military figure and battlefield authority. Use for elite field missions, command disputes, forward operations, and later force-disruption and Community Operation roles.
