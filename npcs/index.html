SOURCE BOUNDARY

Identity does not create hidden mechanics

The source treats peoples, factions, and organizations separately from combat mechanics. A combat-ready NPC uses the nearest approved NPC/family chassis plus only explicit role abilities. Sapient identity does not imply universal hostility, moral alignment, or hidden combat benefits.
