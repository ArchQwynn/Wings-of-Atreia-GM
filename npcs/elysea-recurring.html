SANCTUM

Lavirintos

Source: Aion Adapted · Elyos · Sanctum

Role: Miragent Holy Templar commander and elite-service gatekeeper.

Personality: Formal, proud, disciplined.

Starting Position: Identify Daevas who can be trusted with politically sensitive missions.

GM Secret: He has quietly blocked several brilliant candidates he considers morally unsafe.

Campaign Uses: Elite commissions; disciplinary hearings; missing Miragent patrols.

Relationship Links: Jucleas; Xenophon; Sanctum military offices.

Campaign Role & Development: Phase 0 — Open World: recurring contact for elite commissions, disciplinary hearings, and missing Miragent patrols. Phase I–IV: Tiamat's crisis pushes him toward homeland defense, missing-Daeva response, supply protection, and reinforcement duties; later alliance fracture and Meslamtaeda pressure can shift priorities toward routes, defenses, casualty response, and evacuation. Phase V–VI: Lepharist conflict may place him in protection, investigation, civil-security, or relic-related stories; the Fregion invasion shifts surviving homeland NPCs toward defense and evacuation. Protected revelations remain Director-controlled, while authorized personal or local consequences are reported as persistent changes.
