Asmodians

Category: Daeva/humanoid NPC people.

Combat handling: Use the appropriate underlying NPC/family chassis and only the explicit role abilities selected by the GM.

Dragonbound

Category: Adversary organization.

Combat handling: Members retain their underlying ancestry. Use an appropriate humanoid or Balaur chassis and layer the organization role on top.

Elyos

Category: Daeva/humanoid NPC people.

Combat handling: Use the appropriate underlying NPC/family chassis and only the explicit role abilities selected by the GM.

Lepharist Revolutionaries

Category: Adversary organization.

Combat handling: Lepharists use humanoid/NPC role chassis. Pretor remains a separate artificial Lepharist-created encounter chassis.

Reians

Category: Humanoid NPC people.

Combat handling: Use only verified Reian-specific equipment or abilities alongside the appropriate underlying chassis.

Shugos

Category: Sapient people commonly used in mercantile, technical, social, and scenario roles.

Combat handling: Combat statistics are used only when needed; use the appropriate underlying chassis and explicit role abilities.

GM RULE

No hidden benefits

These categories grant no hidden combat benefits. Sapient identity never implies universal hostility or moral alignment.
