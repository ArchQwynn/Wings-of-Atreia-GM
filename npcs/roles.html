NPC ROLES & CHASSIS

NPC Roles

GM-facing guidance for building and portraying NPCs while keeping identity, faction, and combat mechanics separate.

Underlying Chassis

Use the appropriate underlying NPC/family chassis for the individual being represented. Then apply only explicit role abilities selected by the GM.

For Dragonbound, members retain their underlying ancestry and may use an appropriate humanoid or Balaur chassis. For Lepharists, use humanoid/NPC role chassis; Pretor remains a separate artificial encounter chassis.

Role Boundary

People, faction, appearance, ancestry, and organization membership do not automatically create resistances, immunities, special senses, movement modes, languages, spell-like powers, or other combat packages. Those elements must be explicitly established.

Do not infer hidden mechanics

Sapient identity never implies universal hostility or moral alignment. These NPC categories grant no hidden combat benefits. A combat-ready NPC uses the nearest approved NPC/family chassis plus only explicit role abilities.
