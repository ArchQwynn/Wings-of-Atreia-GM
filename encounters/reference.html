ENCOUNTER REFERENCE

Building Encounters

GM-facing guidance for selecting creature families, ranks, bands, and regional context from the approved Monster Compendium.

Encounter Baseline

Wings of Atreia does not use Challenge Rating or XP encounter budgets. Ordinary creatures are calibrated by PC Band and Rank for a standard party baseline of five PCs.

Creature Selection

Find the creature by family name, select the PC Band and Rank appropriate to the encounter, and use only the statistics and abilities printed in that entry or an explicitly approved variant.

Regional presence is a practical GM lookup rather than an exhaustive range restriction. Migration, campaign events, and explicitly established variants may justify an appearance outside the listed region.

Explicit-Only Traits

Creature type, anatomy, rank, lineage, and appearance do not create hidden mechanics. Resistances, immunities, vulnerabilities, special senses, unusual movement, languages, spell-like powers, save proficiencies, and other traits exist only when explicitly printed.

Sapient Creatures

A sapient creature statblock represents an individual or group that may participate in combat. It does not declare an entire people inherently evil, universally hostile, or automatically a valid combat target.

For NPC peoples and organizations, use the appropriate underlying NPC/family chassis and only explicit role abilities. Identity and faction membership do not create hidden combat packages.

GM QUICK RULE

Use the printed entry

Do not infer mechanics from appearance, lineage, faction, rank, or location. If a trait is not explicitly printed, it is not part of the encounter.
