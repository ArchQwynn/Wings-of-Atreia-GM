ABYSS / RESHANTA

The Abyss

The central gap between Elysea and Asmodae. The GM register for the Abyss and its three Reshanta tiers.

STRUCTURE

Tower Remnant

The surviving Tower remnant is a defining geographic feature of the Abyss. Its approved GM reference places the remnant at approximately 200 km in height with an approximately 500 km footprint.

Lower Reshanta

Approved Visual Structure

The Abyss is a vast void populated by scattered floating rocks and debris rather than one continuous landmass. There are no buildings within the general Abyss field. Reshanta is divided into three clearly separated tiers with large empty voids between them.

Terminon Fortress and Primum Fortress are suspended approximately 2 km above the Tower remnant, at roughly 202 km altitude in the approved geography.
