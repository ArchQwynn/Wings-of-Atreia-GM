ATREIA

World Overview

The established physical structure of Atreia. This page provides the GM with the geographic framework used by the campaign.

The World

Atreia is divided into the southern Elysea and northern Asmodae, with the Abyss occupying the vast central space between them. This GM reference follows the approved Wings of Atreia geography rather than introducing alternate arrangements.

Elysea

Elysea occupies the southern half of Atreia. Its established regional register includes Sanctum/Oriel, Poeta, Verteron, Eltnen, Heiron, and Theobomos.

Asmodae

Asmodae occupies the northern half. Its established regional register includes Pandaemonium/Pernon, Ishalgen, Altgard, Morheim, Beluslan, and Brusthonin.

The Abyss

The Abyss separates the two halves. Its strategic register is organized into Upper Reshanta, the Core or Eye of Reshanta, and Lower Reshanta, with the Tower remnant forming a defining feature of the setting.
