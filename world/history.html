History

GM-facing historical reference. A place for the campaign's established chronology and historical events.

Current Campaign Era

The approved starting era is approximately 500 years after the Deltras incident. The Abyss War has already lasted centuries, and Ereshkigal controls the Abyss.

Starting Political Geography

At campaign start, Elyos control Terminon Fortress and Asmodians control Primum Fortress.
