ASMODAE

Asmodae

Northern half of Atreia. The GM geographic register for Asmodae and its established regions.

Pandaemonium

The Asmodian capital region, paired geographically with Pernon in the established register.

Pernon

The established region associated with Pandaemonium.

Ishalgen

The southern island region of Asmodae.

Altgard

An established central Asmodian region, partially frozen in the approved visual canon.

Morheim

An established western/northern region, heavily frozen in the approved visual canon.

Beluslan

An established northern/western region, heavily frozen in the approved visual canon.

Brusthonin

The western island region of Asmodae.

GM NOTE

Asmodae is cold and dark in the approved WoA visual canon; it is not an infernal realm. Detailed regional lore can be expanded here as canon is finalized.
